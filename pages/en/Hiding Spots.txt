'''Hiding Spots''' are features within a [[Map]] that allow players to survive [[hunt]]s.

==Mechanics==
===Availability===
The number of available hiding spots depends on the [[difficulty]] as well as the number of players:
*'''Amateur:''' All spots available
*'''Intermediate:''' At least 3 spots available
*'''Professional:''' At least 2 spots available
*'''Nightmare/Insanity:''' At least 1 spot available
*'''3 players:''' +1 spot available
*'''4 players:''' +2 spots available
For example, a Professional game with 3 players will have at least 3 hiding spots available throughout the entire map.

By using a custom difficulty, hiding spots can also be turned off entirely, overriding all other rules.

When a spot is unavailable, it can be blocked off with "junk"/storage equipment, be sealed behind a locked door, or have its concealing elements removed. The unavailable spots are randomly chosen at the beginning of each investigation.

===List of hiding spots===
A full list of hiding spots is available on each map's own page. See [[Map#List of maps|Map § List of maps]].

==Tips==
[[File:Ridgeview Kitchen Fridge Unofficial.png|thumb|160x160px|"Unofficial" fridge hiding spot in [[10 Ridgeview Court]]]]
* The ghost cannot open a closet/locker door that is currently being held by a player; Ideally, using this method, the ghost can only open one of the closet doors. Both doors can be held, in multiplayer.
** When hiding in a closet with one door fully open, you must hug the door you are holding to have better chances at survival.
** However, if the ghost detected the player inside or near a closet/locker, the ghost will forcibly open the doors, overriding the player holding the door shut.
* Players are not forced to use "official" hiding spots. Some players may improvise and use furniture such as kitchen tables/islands to hide. As a general rule of thumb, if an object covers the player to at least their eye level, it can (but may not) work as a viable hiding spot.
