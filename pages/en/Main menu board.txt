The '''main menu board''' allows the player to select different game modes (singleplayer/multiplayer lobbies or [[training]]), access [[options]] and view credits. It also shows the given player's [[ID Card]] as well as available [[Daily and Weekly Tasks]].

In the bottom left corner of the board, the player's Unity ID is shown.

!Tip

|Be careful of the dark, the Ghost can sense you 
|The ghost's vision remains the same throughout the game. However, the player's vision is reduced to 1 meter if there is no source of light. This tip most likely encourages players to never take advantage of darkness (particularly during [[hunt]]s).
|-
|Don't stay in the dark too long
| rowspan="2" |Staying in any unlit area drains [[sanity]], even if the player has its own source of light. The only exception to this are [[candle]]s. The lower the sanity, the more likely a ghost is to manifest itself or start hunting.

|Keep your sanity high by staying in the light 
|-
|Ghosts have been known to stalk their victims in the dark
| rowspan="2" |Light does not affect general ghost behaviour other than their likeliness to perform [[interaction]]s. Some ghosts do have a special ability that allows them to "stalk" the player but the lighting does not affect this.
|-
|Ghosts have been known to target those who stay in the light too long

|Listen for distant sounds

|Read your Journal by pressing [J] for more information

|Running isn't always the best option
| Some ghosts move faster than the player can run even without any customizeable buffs; for example, it is impossible to outrun a [[Moroi]] at its maximum speed.
|-
|Use the truck screens to help locate the ghost 
|

|Use your equipment to locate the ghost and find evidence
|

|Watch what you are saying, the Ghosts are listening 
|Ghosts can detect players talking if their voice chat is active, while the [[Yokai]] has a higher hunt sanity threshold if players are talking near it. In addition, saying recognised phrases near all ghosts has a chance of causing them to perform an [[interaction]].
