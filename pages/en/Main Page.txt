<div style="text-align:center">[[Phasmophobia]] is a 4-player, online co-op, psychological horror game, made by [[Kinetic Games]], where you and your team of paranormal investigators will enter [[map|haunted locations]] filled with paranormal activity and try to gather as much [[evidence]] as you can. You will use your ghost-hunting [[equipment]] to find and record evidence to sell on to a ghost removal team.<br><br>
