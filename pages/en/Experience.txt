# 源: https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/api.php'''Experience''' (or '''XP''') in ''Phasmophobia'' is used to unlock various [[equipment]] and [[difficulty|difficulties]].

==Mechanics==
Each player starts off at level 1 with 0 XP, and earns XP through finishing [[contract]]s. The experience required to level up increases progressively, and the level is capped at 9,999. Once level 100 has been reached, the player can choose to Prestige, which will wipe out their level, money, and equipment, in exchange for a new badge and title for the [[ID Card]]. The maximum Prestige level is 3,999.

The ID Card lists the number of levels the player has and the amount of XP required to reach the next level. The amount of XP earned each contract is displayed on the debriefing screen.

To get the additional Perfect Investigation Bonus of 50 XP, the player must identify the correct ghost, complete all 3 objectives, collect the [[Bone Evidence|bone]], and fill the journal with only unique media.

The amount of experience gained from identifying the ghost, completing optional objectives, recorded media, and any investigation bonus(es) is then multiplied by the difficulty multiplier:
*'''Amateur:''' 1x
*'''Intermediate:''' 2x
*'''Professional:''' 3x
*'''Nightmare:''' 4x
*'''Insanity:''' 6x
*'''Custom:''' 0x - 15x depending on settings

If the player does not identify the correct ghost on a custom difficulty, their rewards will be rounded down to the nearest default difficulty (e.g. from 7x to 6x, which is the Insanity multiplier). Difficulty multipliers below 1x are not affected.

If the player is dead when the investigation ends, the difficulty multiplier is not applied if it is higher than 1x, and their experience gained is halved.

If the resulting EXP is not an integer value, it is then rounded down to the nearest integer.

===Level progression===
Players will unlock more equipment, difficulty levels, and maps as they progress.

Prestige level doesn't change the amount of EXP for level up.

==Trivia==
*The theoretical maximum experience you can gain in a single contract (excluding [[Daily and Weekly Tasks]]) is 14,261, by completing a perfect game against an [[Obake]] on the highest difficulty multiplier with Blood Moon weather on [[Map#Large|large maps]]. When additionally completing all the most rewarding daily and weekly tasks during a [[Special events|special event]] (only on the 2nd, 11th, and 25th of the month), the absolute maximum experience is 22,861.
*It is possible to calculate each level's cumulative XP needed. With <math>x</math> being the targeted level:
**If <math>x\leq100</math>, the formula used is <math>100(x-1)^{1.73}</math> rounded down.
**If <math>101\leq x\leq999</math>, the formula used is <math>283432+4971(x-100)</math>.
**If <math>x>999</math>, the formula used is <math>4468929+100(x-900)^{1.73}</math> rounded down.
