'''Sinks''' can be found in various places such as kitchens, toilets and bathrooms on any map. They can be turned on or off by both the player and the ghost.

==Mechanics==
When the ghost interacts with a sink, it will fill the basin with [[Dirty Water|dirty water]], giving out an [[EMF]] 2 reading (with a 33% chance of being [[EMF Level 5|EMF 5]] for the relevant ghosts).

Be mindful that most but not all sinks are functional sinks. One can verify if a sink is functional by {{Control|interacting}} with the tap. If the player cannot turn on the tap for clean water, the ghost cannot use it for dirty water either. An example of a non-functional sink is in the utility room of [[6 Tanglewood Drive]].
