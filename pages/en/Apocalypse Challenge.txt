The '''Apocalypse Challenge''' is a special in-game challenge. The challenge is permanent and will exist indefinitely.

==Details==
The requirements for the challenge are as follows:
*Play in a high [[difficulty]] (see below)
*Play in Singleplayer on [[Sunny Meadows Mental Institution|Sunny Meadows]]
*Complete all 3 optional [[objectives]]
*Capture a ghost [[media]]
*Correctly identify the [[ghost]] type
*Survive the investigation

===Rewards===
Trophies and [[ID_Card#Special_Badges|ID card badges]] will be awarded based on the difficulty multiplier:
*6x multiplier or higher: Bronze trophy, Apocalypse Adept badge
*10x multiplier or higher: Silver trophy, Apocalypse Master badge
*15x multiplier: Gold trophy, Apocalypse Legend badge

Completing a higher multiplier will automatically reward the badges from the lower multipliers; all three badges can be obtained within the same 15x multiplier contract. The awarded trophy will be displayed in the [[lobby]] and automatically upgraded when a higher multiplier is completed.
