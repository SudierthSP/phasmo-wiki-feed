}}'''0.15.0''', also known as the '''Nell's Diner''' update, is a major update released on 11 November 2025.<ref>[https://www.kineticgames.co.uk/news/phasmophobia---nells-diner-v01500 ''Phasmophobia - Nell’s Diner v0.15.0.0''. 11 November 2025.]</ref><ref group="note">Official Kinetic Games Partners were given early access keys on 10 October 2025.</ref> A Steam-exclusive hotfix carrying the same version number was released on 20 November 2025 to fix a crash.<ref>[https://www.kineticgames.co.uk/news/phasmophobia---hotfix-steam-only ''Phasmophobia - Hotfix (STEAM ONLY)''. 20 November 2025.]</ref> This update adds a new small map.

==Additions==
*'''[[Nell's Diner]]:''' Nell's Diner is the newest map of Phasmophobia. Similar in size to 6 Tanglewood Drive, but much denser with a wide array of new interactable objects for both players and ghosts. Consisting of 14 rooms total, with 13 possible ghost rooms - including a walk-in freezer room - there's plenty for Ghost Hunters to discover.
*'''Moneybags:''' We've added the new Moneybags ID Card & Badge with this update. This'll unlock once the player has earned the ‘Break the Bank’ achievement.
**If you have already earned the achievement, this ID Card will unlock upon spending at least $1 in the in game shop and completing your first contract after downloading the update.

==Fixes==
===From versions released before 0.15.0===
*Fixed an issue that caused no audio to play when a light is shattered during a ghost event.
*Fixed an issue that caused Ghost Orbs to go through walls in to other rooms.
*Fixed an issue that caused the Crucifix to fall under the map if placed inside an object.
**In order to fix this, Crucifixes can no longer be placed inside an object.

===From 0.15.0 (as fixed in hotfix)===
*Fixed an issue affecting some players with integrated graphics cards that would cause crashes when loading in to Nell’s Diner.
