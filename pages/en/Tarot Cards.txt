'''Tarot cards''' are a [[Cursed Possession|cursed possession]] that can trigger various randomly chosen effects on both the player or the [[ghost]].

==Mechanics==
[[File:Tarot card draw action.gif|200px|thumb|right|Drawing a ''Death'' card initiates a cursed hunt.]]
As a cursed possession, tarot cards have a 1 in 7 chance per contract of being chosen as the cursed possession to spawn on a default [[difficulty]] (except Insanity), with one specific spawn location per map. 

Tarot cards can be {{Control|picked up}} with an empty hand and one card can be {{Control|drawn}} at a time. Tarot cards can only be drawn while inside the investigation area.

Each Tarot cards deck contains 10 randomly generated cards. There are 10 possible card types the player can draw, each having a unique effect on either the player or the ghost. After being used, each card will burn up and vanish in a uniquely-colored flame. Each card type has a different, independent chance of being drawn, so some card types can be drawn multiple times or not at all in a single deck.

Once all ten cards have been used, no more will be given to the player for that contract. When the deck is used up, all future hunts will also be extended by 20 seconds, even if no <span style="color: #8400c3;">Death</span> card was drawn prior.<ref group="note">This is very likely a bug.</ref>

==Tips==
*The player should not draw a card during a hunt, since all cards drawn during one have a 100% chance to be <span style="color: #ba48e0;">The Fool</span>, essentially wasting the card.
*Though tarot cards cannot be drawn outside of the investigation area, this can be bypassed by drawing a card and walking outside of an [[Exit Door|exit door]] during the card-pulling animation. Cards will still take effect.
**While using this method, as long as no players are inside: drawing <span style="color: #f25277;">The Devil</span> will do nothing, and drawing <span style="color: #8400c3;">Death</span> will not trigger a hunt but will still extend the duration of later hunts.
*Players should take a photo of the cards before using them, as once all of the cards are drawn, the deck will no longer be an item and no photo can be taken.
*Trying to take a ghost photo after drawing <span style="color: #f25277;">The Devil</span> can sometimes be tricky, since the card's burning animation needs to be completed before the player can drop the deck, so multiplayer coordination is a good solution to this.
**This is more easily done if the ghost decides to perform a singing ghost event and doesn't spawn too close to the targeted player, as singing ghost events usually last around 10 - 15 seconds.<!--Rough estimate, feel free to change.-->

==Trivia==
*On Steam, the Tarot Cards designs are also used for the game's Trading Cards, which can be purchased and sold on the Community Market.
*Tarot Cards are the only cursed possession that is not guaranteed to cause a cursed hunt, as it is possible for the entire deck to not contain a single <span style="color: #8400c3;">Death</span> (roughly 34.9% chance); this can prove to be detrimental if the player wants to cause a hunt to aid in completing objectives and/or identifying the ghost.
*Though ''extremely'' unlikely (1/10,000,000,000 decks), it is theoretically possible to draw <span style="color: #8400c3;">Death</span> 10 times in a row, making Tarot Cards the cursed possession that can cause the most cursed hunts, up to 10 cursed hunts if the player is extraordinarily (un)lucky.
