}}'''0.5.0.1''' is a minor update to the game released on 10 December 2021.<ref>https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/3099036942813472786</ref> The update includes bug fixes.

== Bug fixes ==
*Fixed a bug where the Mimic had a 20% chance to spawn. It is now double the chance of other ghosts
*Fixed a bug where you couldn’t take a photo of the Maple lamp post fingerprints
*Fixed a bug where the Cursed Possessions breaking would increase the hunt duration significantly in multiplayer
*Fixed a bug where VR players couldn’t see other VR players
