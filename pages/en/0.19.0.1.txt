}}'''0.19.0.1''' is a minor update released on 10 September 2026.<ref>[https://www.kineticgames.co.uk/news/phasmophobia-v01901-patch-notes ''Phasmophobia v0.19.0.1 - Patch Notes''. 10 September 2026.]</ref> It fixes several issues.

==Additions==
*Added a VR Belt Offset option to adjust the height to be either lower or higher.
*Added more props and fingerprint interactions to Prison Entrance to assist with Ghost identification.

==Changes==
*Adjusted the position of the VR player’s hand to be a more comfortable position for the following equipment:
**Tier 1 D.O.T.S Projector.
**All Tiers of Flashlights.
**All Tiers of Photo Cameras.
**All Tiers of Video Cameras.
**More equipment will be improved with future updates.

==Fixes==
*Fixed an issue that caused several cosmetic items to cover the Watch.
**''Note: The Female Hooded Denim jacket will still overlap the Watch slightly. This will be addressed in a future update.''
*Fixed an issue that caused several achievements to complete despite the player being on a custom difficulty with a multiplier of 0.
*Fixed an issue that caused correct Evidence to be crossed out after using the ‘I Wish For Knowledge’ wish with the Monkey Paw.
*Fixed an issue that caused various Ouija Board animations and interactions to break if the player opened the Journal during an answer.
*Fixed an issue that prevented the Video Camera from being picked up off a Tripod by other players if another player had placed the Tripod.
*Fixed an issue that prevented the Nell’s Diner safe from opening despite the correct code being entered.
*Fixed an issue that caused Hiding Spots on Prison Restricted being unblocked despite No Hiding Spots being set.
*Fixed an issue that caused a Video Camera’s screen to remain black for an entire contract if it was opened in the Truck before opening the door.
*Fixed an issue that caused Video Camera screens to remain black if headgear was toggled while the screen was blacked out.
*Fixed an issue that caused Video Camera screens to remain black if the journal was opened while the camera was inside a wall.
*Fixed an issue that caused the player to spawn into a game as Amir Kelly with default cosmetics equipped, despite having another character or cosmetics selected.
*Fixed an issue that caused the Bone to spawn in locked Bathroom Stalls in Brownstone High School Restricted maps.
*Fixed an issue that caused the Fusebox to spawn in an inaccessible location on Prison Restricted.
*Fixed an issue that caused character animations to break if the player cycled out or dropped a Tier 1 Photo Camera during the Use animation.
*Fixed an issue that caused the player to become stuck on the chair in Classroom 6 of Brownstone High School.
*Fixed an issue on PlayStation 5 and Xbox consoles that caused players to be unable to use Voice Recognition if they had previously switched from Voice to Text, then back to Voice.
*Fixed an issue that caused Equipment to be lost if placed on certain blockades of the new Restricted maps.
*Fixed an issue that caused a double click on Ghost Selection in custom difficulty if using a controller.
*Fixed an issue that prevented UV Fingerprints on 13 Willow Street and Nell’s Diner from being charged, preventing Photos being taken.
*Fixed an issue that caused a door’s audio to not play correctly if a Ghost closed it.
*Fixed an issue that caused Igniters to turn off if the player grabs a Firelight.
*Fixed an issue that caused VR player profile pictures to be blank when they join a lobby.
*Fixed an issue that caused the Monkey Paw to spawn in the incorrect spot on Prison Restricted.
*Fixed an issue for VR players where their Head Gear could fall off if another player closed a door on them.
*Fixed an issue that caused headgear ownership to change when a PC and VR player are both interacting with the same door.
*Fixed an issue where the player could hear the audio of the Journal opening when dying.
*Fixed an issue that showed the incorrect room blocked off on the polaroid for Point Hope Restricted.
*Fixed a visual issue causing part of the ground on Point Hope to appear white.
*Fixed an issue in the Journal where a highlighted Evidence type would remain highlighted after closing the Journal.
*Fixed an issue that caused duplicate player ID Cards to display on the lobby board when players join a session.
*Fixed a visual issue with jackets on female character models that would show gaps in the equipped cosmetic.
*Fixed an issue that caused poor performance on High School Restricted.
*Fixed an issue that caused player audio to be muffled in Multiplayer if another player dropped the Parabolic Microphone immediately after turning it on.
*Fixed an issue to prevent hackers from altering evidence money reward values.
*VR - Fixed an issue affecting PSVR2 users that meant the UI pointer was titled downwards, needing the player to tilt their hand upwards to interact with the UI.
*VR - Fixed an issue affecting PSVR2 where the player character would not move from the centre of the play space.
*VR - Fixed an issue that caused dead bodies to contort upon death if the player had Immersive Animations turned on.

==Known Issues==
*Localised text for Evidence captured with a Video Camera may not display correctly for non-English players.
*Enabling FidelityFX Super Resolution 1.0 causes readability issues with the Journal.
*Can't light placed Tier 1 Firelights with a lit firelight in your hand.
*Firelights don’t blow out when thrown after they are cycled into the inventory.
*Boombox in the lobby doesn't always turn on.
*There's new safe spots in the Restricted maps caused by hiding behind the new objects blocking doors and hallways.
*VR - Tier 1 D.O.T.S Projector can cause the character’s hand animation to rotate in unnatural ways.
*Common crashes for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.
