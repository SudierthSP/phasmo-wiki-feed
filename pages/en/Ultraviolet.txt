Ultraviolet is a type of [[evidence]] created by [[ghost]]s that can be used to determine the ghost type.

==Mechanics==
Ultraviolet evidence consists of fingerprints and footprints which can be revealed by using a [[UV Light]].

===Fingerprints===
[[File:Fingerprint Patterns 1.png|200x200px|thumb|The six patterns of fingerprints, including the Obake's double fingerprints.]]
[[File:Fingerprint Patterns 2.png|200x200px|thumb|The six patterns of handprints, including the Obake's six-fingered handprint.]]
[[File:Fingerprint Patterns 3.png|200x200px|thumb|The six patterns of four fingerprints, including the Obake's five fingerprints.]]
By default, each [[interaction]] on a valid surface will always produce a fingerprint. Exceptions include with the [[Obake]] which has a 25% chance to hide them, or when using the "Fingerprint chance" custom difficulty modifier to reduce the odds.

A ghost with Ultraviolet evidence will leave behind fingerprints when it interacts with the following surfaces or items:
*Most types of [[door]]s
*[[Light]] switches and lamps
*Windows
*Keyboards
*TV remotes
*Cooler boxes
*Mirrors (specific maps only)

The [[Prison]] map has additional places where fingerprints may be left behind:
*On the horizontal surface of a bar of cell doors
*On the steel keyhole surface of cell doors

[[Maple Lodge Campsite]] and [[Camp Woodwind]] also have additional places where fingerprints may appear:
*Bases of lanterns
*Interior edges around large tent door flaps/tops of small tent door flaps
*Picnic benchtops
*String light poles

Lights flickering or interactions caused by [[ghost event]]s will not result in a fingerprint being left behind in these instances.

Depending on the surface area of the object being interacted with, either a fingerprint or a handprint will be visible. In addition, the print will always appear in the same spot for that specific object.

Fingerprints will disappear completely after 2 minutes, or 1 minute on Insanity [[difficulty]]. This can be changed using a custom [[difficulty]] setting. Additionally, the Obake can sometimes use an ability to reduce the remaining time for all existing fingerprints on the map by half.

When leaving fingerprints on surfaces it touches, an [[Obake]] has an approximately 16.7% chance to leave a non-standard pattern:
*A handprint with six fingers instead of five
*Two fingerprints instead of one on light switches, lamps, and TV remotes
*Five fingerprints instead of four on keyboards and Prison doors

===Footprints===
[[File:Footprints 0.4.0.png|thumb|256x256px|Footprints lit up by a tier II UV light.]]
[[File:Footprints vary by size.png|thumb|A comparison of footprints left by different ghost models.]]
When a ghost crosses a [[Salt|salt pile]] and disturbs it, it will leave a footprint with each step until it stops moving or for a maximum of 2 seconds, leading to 4 footprints, and each footprint will produce a footstep sound and disappear after 10 to 15 seconds. If the ghost doesn't have Ultraviolet as evidence, or the evidence is hidden, its footprints will be invisible.
*The [[Gallu]] in its weakened state can leave footprints for a maximum of 3 seconds, leading to 6 footprints instead of 4.
*The Obake has a 25% chance each step to hide the footprint, creating no footstep sound when successful.

Because of the random nature of the game, a ghost may step in a salt pile and choose to stop moving immediately, creating no footprints in that instance.

Footprints left by the "lamprey-faced" child [[ghost model]] are slightly smaller than those left by other ghost models.

===Rewards===
{{Main|Media}}
Taking a photo of a fingerprint or a footprint that has been charged using a [[UV Light]] will respectively yield a photo labelled as '''Fingerprint''' or '''Footprint''', each rewarding up to $7 and 7 XP, depending on the tier of the [[Photo Camera]] and whether there is a Unique stamp. Keep in mind, however, that the prints will slowly fade before becoming completely invisible after the player stops charging them with a UV Light. Invisible prints cannot be photographed and will result in an invalid photo. Each instance of a fingerprint, including repeat fingerprints on the same surface, can only be photographed once.

==Tips==
*Footprints can be useful to check for Ultraviolet evidence if the ghost is not interacting with doors in a timely fashion.
*Footprints can sometimes be covered by certain furniture or carpets.
*During a [[hunt]], the ghost will almost certainly open and/or close a few doors; scanning doors with a UV Light immediately after a hunt ends can often reveal some fingerprints if the ghost has Ultraviolet as one of its evidence.

==Trivia==
*Exit doors can show a fingerprint. For this to happen, the ghost must be [[The Mimic]], it must be mimicking a [[Yurei]], it must use the door-closing ability on the exit door, and the Ultraviolet evidence is not hidden. Hence, this occurrence is very rare.

==Related difficulty settings==
*'''Fingerprint chance (%):''' The chance of a ghost leaving fingerprints when interacting (only shows when ''Evidence given'' is not 0)
*'''Fingerprint duration (s):''' The duration of fingerprints before they disappear (only shows when ''Evidence given'' is not 0)
