The '''Monkey Paw'''  is a [[Cursed Possession|cursed possession]] that can be used to grant the player wishes.

==Mechanics==
A Monkey Paw has a 1 in 7 chance per contract of being chosen as the cursed possession that will spawn on a default [[difficulty]] (except Insanity), with one specific spawn location per map. The Monkey Paw grants up to a certain number of wishes depending on the difficulty reward multiplier:
*0x – 1x: 5 wishes
*1.01x – 2x: 4 wishes
*2.01x or higher: 3 wishes

The Monkey Paw can only be used within the investigation area, and players can choose to either use voice chat or a text-based UI to make wishes.
*If using voice chat, the Monkey Paw will grant any recognized wish made by the player.
*If using the text-based UI, the UI will appear upon pressing the {{Control|Primary Use}} button. Players can then click to select one of 3 categories, then select a category-specific wish, or exit the UI by pressing the {{Control|Primary Use}} button again.
Every wish will come with a negative side effect, with the severity depending on the wish.

If the Monkey Paw recognizes a valid phrase, regardless if it is a valid wish or not, or when entering/exiting the investigation area, its fingers will twitch. When a wish is being granted, one of its fingers will bend. When all fingers are bent, the Monkey Paw cannot be used anymore.

Most wishes can be found on tags scattered in [[Sunny Meadows]], these tags look the same as the one found on a Monkey Paw. Picking a tag up (except for "I wish for [weather]" and "I wish for anything" tags, as well as the Monkey Paw's own tag) will record the wish in the journal. There is no need to find the wishes to use the Monkey Paw. Each wish can only be used once per contract, regardless of the wisher.

====Miscellaneous====
Due to the game's voice recognition system, some sentences may be interpreted as valid wishes, including silly wishes.

Examples include "I wish for infinite wishes" which may be misheard as "I wish for sun". Sometimes the wishes "I wish to be sane" and "I wish to be safe"  can be misheard as the other wish.

===Rewards===
{{Main|Media}}
Taking a photo of a Monkey Paw, regardless of the number of uses, will yield a photo labelled as '''Monkey Paw''', and reward up to $7 and 7 XP, depending on the tier of the [[Photo Camera]].

==Tips==
*The Monkey Paw is one of the most versatile cursed possessions in the game, and can assist players in the completion of most [[objectives]]. The various wishes can be called upon on command at any time, meaning that players can control when and where to employ their effects to maximize the benefits and minimize the downsides.
**In multiplayer, the Monkey Paw becomes even more powerful than it already is in singleplayer, as a lot of wish downsides only affect the wisher.
*One of the safety wish's downsides, allowing the ghost to detect the wisher from any distance, while significant on paper, can actually be used to the wisher's benefit to bait the ghost to wherever the wisher is, whether to save a teammate, or to lead it to a favourable looping spot for hunt identification and/or photos.
*The knowledge wish becomes slightly more useful in reduced-evidence difficulties, as this wish always eliminates an incorrect evidence type and never a hidden evidence type, making it the only method to guarantee that an evidence type is incorrect. Its harsh downsides, however, make this wish a last resort and should only ever be used near the end of a contract.
*The weather wish can be used if the current weather (such as Blood Moon) is not desired, or to change the ambient temperature to aid in getting [[Freezing Temperatures]] or slowing a [[Hantu]] down, which is especially useful if the fuse box is broken or the map is an outdoors map.
*If not using the text UI, the Monkey Paw is always active when held. Be careful when speaking while holding it as you may accidentally call upon a wish.
*The vision loss from certain wishes is very significant, making it near impossible for a player afflicted by it to see more than a meter or so in front of them. While this can be partially circumvented by setting game brightness to a very high value, the affected player will be more or less rendered helpless and will need to be guarded accordingly. Thus, it is recommended for the wisher to use these wishes while in a hiding spot.

==Trivia==
*The Monkey Paw was [https://www.makeship.com/products/cursed-monkey-paw-plushie available as official merchandise] for a limited time, until 3 July 2024 (UK time).
*When wishing for sanity, the ghost doesn't teleport immediately to the new ghost room, but will have to enter the "Favourite room" state to walk to its new room, which usually happens shortly after wishing. Tier III [[salt]] can be used to deter this.
*The safety wish technically makes the ghost able to detect the player through voice or electronics within 1,000 metres. This wiki uses the phrase "any distance" since there is no map where the player can be 1,000 metres away from the ghost.
*The idea of a "Monkey's Paw" being used to grant wishes is very likely inspired by the short story [[wikipedia:The Monkey's Paw|The Monkey's Paw]] by [[wikipedia:W. W. Jacobs|W. W. Jacobs]].
