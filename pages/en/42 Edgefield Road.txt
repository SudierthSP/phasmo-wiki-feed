}}'''42 Edgefield Road''' (or simply '''Edgefield''') is a small [[map]] in ''Phasmophobia''. It is a two story, six-bedroom house with a basement, with a congested, claustrophobic environment. Despite the thin hallways, there are plenty of hiding spaces during a [[hunt]], as a room to hide in is always a few paces away.

Its tight hallways can often make it difficult to see past fellow investigators, and a team member may be encouraged to set up [[Video Camera]]s and observe key investigative details from the [[truck]].

==Structure==
Edgefield contains six bedrooms (including a nursery), six toilets, a dining room, a garage, and a basement, among other rooms.

===Layout tips===
*Both the basement hallway and the storage room separated by a door is considered a single "Basement" room.
*For every bedroom on the second floor, the game considers both the room with the bed and the attached bathroom, if any, to be a single room.
*There is a bug where the computer in Small Blue Bedroom needs to be turned on to prevent passive sanity drain in that room.

The following rooms allow short [[Roaming|roams]] to another floor to be completed:
*Basement

Several more unofficial furniture-based hiding spots are available. While they are more risky, they will never be blocked unlike official hiding spots, and can still provide decent hiding in most cases. Examples include:
*'''Hallway''', behind a cupboard at the rear end of the hallway
*'''Living Room''', behind the bookshelf. It will block line-of-sight from the doorway, but it can be very risky if the ghost enters the living room.
*'''Basement''', behind the shelves in the storage room

[[Guides/Surviving a hunt#Looping%20the%20ghost|Looping]] the ghost is only possible around the living room couch, but the latter doesn't block line of sight. Using tier III [[Salt]] can be useful to slow down the ghost if one wants to test for certain ghosts (especially [[Poltergeist]] and [[Obake]]).

==Bone locations==
Bones spawn randomly, but will only spawn in areas that a ghost can also walk on. Additionally, the bone cannot spawn in '''Hallway'''.

==Trivia==
*Before the Purple Bedroom's toilet had a sink, it was up to the toilet user to decide how to wash their hands, if at all.
*There used to be a bug where taking a photo of the trash can at the front of the house resulted in a Dead Body photo.
*Since the [[truck]] is parked rather close to the house, tier II and III [[crucifix]]es might get used while they are still hanging in the truck if the ghost is in the Master Bedroom, as their range will reach into the house. If the ghost is a [[Demon]], this might even happen with a tier I crucifix.
*The assets used for this map can be found [https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/urban/hq-residential-house-48976 here].
