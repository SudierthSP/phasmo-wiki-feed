The '''dowsing rods''' are an optional piece of [[equipment]] used to locate the [[Bone Evidence|bone]].

==Mechanics==
When the dowsing rods are being held, they will slowly move back and forth in parallel. The rods will start twitching and stop being parallel when within a certain range of the bone (including through floors) inside the investigation area. As they get even closer to the bone, the rods will cross, at first only when facing the bone, then permanently regardless of the facing direction if the bone is close enough.

<br>For tiers I and II rods, they additionally stay crossed regardless of the facing direction if the bone is within the bone detection range ''and'' in the same room.

Tiers II and III rods will tilt upwards or downwards to indicate whether the bone is located at a floor above or below the player's current floor.

Players will need to stand still for the rods to sense the bone, unless they use the tier III rods.

During [[hunt]]s, the dowsing rods will stop sensing the bone to sense the [[ghost]] instead. The range at which the rods detect the ghost is larger than for the bone:
*Tier I: 22 metres
*Tier II: 34 metres
*Tier III: 45 metres
