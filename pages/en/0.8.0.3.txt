}}'''0.8.0.3''' is a minor hotfix released on 20 December 2022.<ref>[https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/6331529188469231692 ''Tempest | Hotfix v0.8.0.3''. 20 December 2022 (on Steam).]</ref> The update was re-released shortly after to fix an issue with overlapping UI.

== Changes ==
=== Difficulty ===
* Custom Reward Multipliers will now have a maximum cap of x15
** Actual cap will be displayed with the actual multiplier beside it in brackets
* Incorrectly identifying the ghost type will round down your multiplier to the next default difficult
** E.g. 5x > 4x or 3.7x > 3x

=== Equipment ===
;[[Ouija Board]]
* Increased sanity drain for certain questions
** Ghost location: 40% to 50%
** Bone location: 20% to 50%

;[[Haunted Mirror]]
* Will now drain minimum of 20% sanity per use

;[[Smudge Sticks]]
* Will now visually animate much more accurately with the timing of their gameplay effect
* Improved smoke effect

=== Gameplay ===
* Adjusted player freezing breath visuals to no longer block your view when walking too close to it
* Adjusted several inconsistent text capitalizations in English 
* Rebaked Sunny Meadows

== Bug fixes ==
* Removed several sky light leaks in the following locations:
** Willow
** Tanglewood
** Edgefield
** Ridgeview
** Grafton
** Sunny Meadows
* The DOTS and death room ghost will no longer be the wrong ghost model
* The reset save button will now work
* Sanity will now stop draining when you are in a lit area
* Snow will no longer fall inside the Bleasdale attic
* Willow front room window fingerprints will now be photographable
