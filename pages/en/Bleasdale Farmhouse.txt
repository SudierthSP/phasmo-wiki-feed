Bleasdale Farmhouse is a medium [[map]] in ''Phasmophobia'', and one of the two Farmhouse variants, alongside [[Grafton Farmhouse]]. With two well-defined main floors, and a spacious attic, Bleasdale can feel quite big, but still plenty manageable for a small team.

==Structure==
Bleasdale Farmhouse has three floors, including an attic. Rooms consist of two bedrooms, three bathrooms, an office, two hallways, and outdoors areas, among others.

===Layout tips===
*The investigation area includes both the house itself, as well as its backyard and the garden.
*There are motion-triggered lights around the outside of the house, which can be triggered not only by players, but also the ghost. These lights still count towards the light limit rule and the [[Spirit Box]].
*The bead curtain separating the Attic Space from the Attic Bedroom will move when players, dead or alive, or the ghost passes through.
*For Attic Space and Garden, the small lantern also needs to be turned on in addition to the main light to completely prevent passive sanity drain in these rooms.

The following rooms cannot be chosen as a [[Ghost Room|favourite room]]:
*Attic Hallway

==Bone locations==
Bones spawn randomly, but will only spawn in areas that a ghost can also walk on, within a room that can be chosen as a [[Ghost Room|favourite room]].

==Trivia==
*A small spider can sometimes spawn inside the house at different locations: in the stairs in Attic Hallway, in the corner near the open suitcase in Attic Space, in the corner behind the broken cupboard in Attic Space, and above the display cases in Attic Bedroom. When a player approaches the spider, the latter will flee before disappearing. The spider's name is "Lilith" as revealed by a photo below a broken display case in Attic Bedroom.
*A wolf can sometimes be heard through the [[Parabolic Microphone]] on this map.
*A matchbox containing a singular match can be found in the Study.
