'''Daily Tasks''' and '''Weekly Tasks''' are additional goals that provide bonus income when completed. Most tasks on a given day or week are the same for every player. Four daily and weekly tasks each are given at a time.

==Progress==
Both types of tasks can be viewed on the [[main menu board]], and on clipboards that can be found in the [[truck]] (when in a [[contract]]) and the lobby. Where applicable, the task will be listed on the post-game results screen when it is completed during the contract.

Contracts with a [[difficulty]] multiplier of 0x will not count towards any task progress. For [[media]]-related tasks, only pieces of media in the [[journal]] will count.
