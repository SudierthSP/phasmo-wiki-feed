An '''interaction''' is a core mechanic in ''Phasmophobia''. It is an occurrence of the [[ghost]] affecting its environment and items within it, and is one of the key parts of [[Activity|ghost activity]]. Although not [[evidence]] in and of itself, it both helps players to find certain pieces of evidence, and allows them to gather [[Media|rewards]] by recording it.

[[Ghost Event|Ghost events]] and [[hunt]]s do not count as interactions in and of themselves.

==Mechanics==
Through the [[activity]] check, ghosts can enter the interaction state.

Most ghosts can interact with items located within a box<ref>[https://www.twitch.tv/eblavender/clip/SarcasticSullenGorillaM4xHeh-4SPmVXUd6n69tWoH Lavender describing the interaction range as a box (on Twitch). 27 February 2025.]</ref> centered at 1 metre above their feet. This box rotates with the ghost's facing direction, making the effective range a cylinder with a radius of 2.12 meters on small and medium [[map]]s (3m × 3m × 2m box), and 4.24 meters on large maps (6m × 6m × 4m box). Ghosts can interact with objects through walls and through floors, provided these objects are within range.

Each interaction causes an [[EMF]] 2 reading, or an EMF 3 reading in the case of a thrown object, emitted at the point of interaction for 20 seconds. If an object levitates, there is an additional EMF 3 that only lasts for the duration of levitation. If the ghost has [[EMF Level 5]] as one of its types of evidence, each individual reading has a 33% chance to be EMF 5 instead. When touching surfaces or switches, the relevant ghosts may also leave [[Ultraviolet#Fingerprints|fingerprints]] on them.

Interactions can be forced by using a [[Voodoo Doll]].
