The '''computer''' is an [[equipment]] located on a desk in the [[truck]].

==Usage==
By default, the computer displays a screensaver, but when the player approaches it, it displays a live feed of any currently-active camera on the map, including [[video camera]]s, Tier I [[Head Gear]], and the location's [[Video Feed|video feed]].

When {{Control|interacting}} with the computer, players can then select which camera to focus on, or use one of the available buttons at the bottom of the screen. The REC button will start the video recording for the selected camera, the PAN buttons will rotate the camera if placed on a [[tripod]], the NV button will toggle night vision, and the ZOOM buttons will adjust the zooming.

The use of the computer in tandem with video cameras allows for the safe gathering of some [[evidence]] types: [[Ghost Orb]]s, [[Ghost Writing]], [[Ultraviolet]] and [[D.O.T.S. Projector]] can all be observed through the screen with proper positioning of the camera and the relevant equipment.
