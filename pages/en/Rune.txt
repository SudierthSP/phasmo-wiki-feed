'''Runes''' were part of riddles that lasted between [[0.8.1.0]] and [[0.10.0]]. Using the [[UV Light]] in the [[lobby]], the player could find the runes on the ground near the display cases.

Solving the riddles allowed the player to get the name of a new map which ultimately was [[Point Hope]].

== Riddle locations ==
<gallery>
Bleasdale Riddle.png|The riddle in [[Bleasdale Farmhouse]] was located in the stairs leading to the Attic, on the side of the Attic's floor.
Camp Woodwind Riddle.png|The riddle in [[Camp Woodwind]] was located in the Food Tent, behind some boxes in the left-hand corner.
Edgefield Riddle.png|The riddle in [[42 Edgefield Road]] was located on a barrier next to the [[truck]].
Prison Riddle.png|The riddle in [[Prison]] was located on the left side of the truck.
Ridgeview Riddle.png|The riddle in [[10 Ridgeview Court]] was located on the wall of the house, on the right of the [[Exit Door|front door]].
Tanglewood Riddle.png|The riddle in [[6 Tanglewood Drive]] was located in the Master Bedroom's closet, behind the door.
Willow Riddle.png|The riddle in [[13 Willow Street]] was located in Boys Bedroom, on a whiteboard next to the computer.
</gallery>

== Riddle solutions ==
<gallery>
Bleasdale Riddle Solution.png|Bleasdale Farmhouse's rune could be found in [[Grafton Farmhouse]], when lighting a flame near a scarecrow while the weather is either Light Rain or Snow.
Camp Woodwind Riddle Solution.png|Camp Woodwind's rune could be found in [[13 Willow Street]], when pointing a UV light through the window in Master Bedroom towards the wall of the staircase while the weather is Fog.
Edgefield Riddle Solution.png|42 Edgefield Road's rune could be found in [[6 Tanglewood Drive]], when pointing a UV light towards the ceiling, above the rocking chair in the basement.
Prison Riddle Solution.png|Prison's rune could be found in [[Brownstone High School]], after following a "maze" of arrows with a UV light from the right back exit door.
Ridgeview Riddle Solution.png|10 Ridgeview Court's rune could be found in [[Prison]], when pointing a UV light on a wall in Bathroom B.
Tanglewood Riddle Solution.png|6 Tanglewood Drive's rune could be found in [[Maple Lodge Campsite]], when pointing a UV light to the ground, at the end of the Pier while the weather is Sunrise.
Willow Riddle Solution.png|13 Willow Street's rune could be found in [[Sunny Meadows]], when pointing a UV light on a boiler in Boilerhouse while the weather is Snow.
</gallery>
