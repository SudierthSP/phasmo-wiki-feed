}}'''0.2.10.1''' is a minor update to the game released on 8 July 2021.<ref>https://discord.com/channels/435431947963990026/661365069006897154/862644015869460503</ref> This update makes several minor changes to gameplay.

== Changes ==
=== Ghosts ===
*[[Hantu]] and [[Yokai]] no longer have an increased chance to spawn

=== Maps ===
*[[Willow Street House]] will no longer always show on the contract selection map

=== General ===
*The voice chat system has been changed from Photon Voice to Dissonance
*The versioning system has been slightly changed to improve clarity
