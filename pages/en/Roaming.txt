# 源: https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/api.phpThe '''roaming''' is a core mechanic in ''Phasmophobia''. It is one of the states the [[ghost]] can choose to make the investigation harder by being more unpredictable. In this state, it essentially moves to another spot within the [[map]].

==Mechanics==
When the ghost enters a roaming state, it will first have a 1/3 chance (2/3 chance for [[Goryo]]) to enter a [[D.O.T.S. Projector (Evidence)|D.O.T.S state]] instead of the actual roaming state if it has D.O.T.S. Projector as evidence. If it does enter the actual roaming state, it will choose to either perform a short or a long roam. The chance of each is based on the "Roaming frequency" difficulty setting:

<br>After having chosen which type of roam it will perform, the ghost will pick a random waypoint within a spherical area. That sphere has a radius of 3 metres for short roams, and a random radius between 5 and 11 metres for long roams.

The ghost then checks the pathing distance to that waypoint and the roam will succeed only if the pathing distance is less or equal than 150% of the direct distance, else it will pick another waypoint and retry.<ref>[https://clips.twitch.tv/InventivePlausibleGiraffeTakeNRG-BwqV8FU99njdrNUY ''How ghosts roam.'' Clipped from cjdxn (on Twitch). 10 July 2022.]</ref>

The short roam will also fail if the waypoint is located on another floor unless the ghost's current room allows it to roam to another floor (e.g. single-room basement/attic, some rooms on [[Point Hope]], Cabin Bedroom on [[Maple Lodge Campsite]]). The long roam will also fail if the ghost is not in its [[Ghost Room|favourite room]]. Both types of roam will fail if the waypoint is located two or more floors away from the favourite room.

===Cursed possessions===
When "The Hermit" [[Tarot Cards|tarot card]] is drawn or when the [[Monkey Paw]]'s "I wish to trap the ghost" wish is used, every roaming state will be replaced by a [[Ghost Room|favourite room]] state for 90 seconds and 60.5 seconds, respectively.

==Tips==
*When [[sanity]] is high, ghosts are more likely to enter the roaming state as they are less likely to perform [[interaction]]s, [[Ghost Event|ghost events]], or abilities, and vice versa.

==Related difficulty settings==
*'''Roaming frequency:''' Low/Medium/High
