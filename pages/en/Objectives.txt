# 源: https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/api.phpPlayers are assigned (optional) '''objectives''' during their investigations. Each [[contract]] has 4 objectives, one of which is always "Find the correct ghost type", and the other three are randomly chosen.

Objectives are displayed on the [[Objective Board|objective board]] and in the [[journal]] (except for "Find the correct ghost type"). The objectives provided are entirely optional, even ignored altogether (forfeiting [[experience]] and [[money|cash]] rewards from them), and can be done in any order. Some of them cannot be completed with only starter [[equipment]].

==Removed objectives ==
*Take a photo of [[Dirty Water]]: removed due to the very low probability of this specific event happening during a game.
*Get a [[temperature]] below 10°C with a [[Thermometer]]: removed due to the changes made to the temperature mechanic.
*Detect Ghost footsteps with a parabolic mic during a hunt: this objective was limited to some beta test versions.
*Get a Ghost to walk through Salt: this was removed due to changes made to the [[Wraith]].
