The '''Sanity Monitor''' is a screen in the [[truck]] labelled "Team Sanity". It reads both the [[sanity]] of individual [[player]]s as well as the average sanity of the team, both expressed as a percentage.

The Sanity Monitor is not perfectly accurate, as it updates every 2 seconds and displays a maximum fluctuation of ±2% per player instead of their actual sanity. Despite this inherent inaccuracy, it is still critical to gleaming player sanity levels, and by that extent seeing how aggressive the [[ghost]] will be; as average sanity decreases, [[hunt]]s will occur with increasing frequency.

Players who are yet to load into the map show up on the Sanity Monitor with their name shown as "Loading...", and a hyphen in place of their sanity. [[Death|Dead]] players will have their sanity value replaced with a "?" instead. Players who have left the game will disappear from the sanity monitor entirely.

In Nightmare and Insanity [[difficulty|difficulties]], the Sanity Monitor is damaged, and will give random readings, becoming essentially useless. Use of an [[Ouija Board]], if available, is thus the only feasible way to get a good estimate of one's current sanity.
