==Throwing mechanics==
Objects are thrown with a "power" of 1 - 3 horizontally, 1 - 5 vertically, and in one of four ways:
*40% chance of direct throw.
*35% chance of levitation then throw.
*20% chance of levitation then drop.
*5% chance of levitation then throw at player.
**The [[Shade]] will fail this action.
These chances also apply to the [[Poltergeist]]'s ability.

Levitations last for 0.5 - 2 seconds normally and for 1 - 3 seconds during a Poltergeist's ability.

Ghosts cannot throw items if the lights in their current room are on,<ref>[https://i.imgur.com/MtFiSs3.png ''<nowiki>"yeah it can't throw anything [with the room lights turned on]"</nowiki>'' Dknighter, ''Phasmophobia'' Lead Developer. 4 December 2022.]</ref> except for when the Poltergeist uses its multithrow ability or when a [[Voodoo Doll]] is used.

The thrown object emits an [[EMF]] 3 reading that lasts for 20 seconds. It emits an additional EMF 3 reading for the duration of the levitation, if any. Both of these readings have a 33% chance to be [[EMF Level 5]] readings instead if the ghost has this evidence.

===Rewards===
{{Main|Media}}
Thrown objects can be filmed using a [[Video Camera|video camera]], which will yield an '''Object Thrown''' video, rewarding up to $7 and 7 XP depending on the equipment tier and whether there is a Unique stamp. While the object levitates, a [[Photo Camera|photo camera]] can take an '''Interaction''' photo, also rewarding up to $7 and 7 XP.
