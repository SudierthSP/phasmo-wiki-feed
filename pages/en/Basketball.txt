'''Basketball''' is a minigame in the [[lobby]]. Two balls and a basket are provided on the second floor behind the spawn area, along with an LCD scoreboard starting at zero. When a [[player]] makes a basket, the scoreboard updates. Basketball does not serve any purpose other than a pastime when waiting for players to ready up and start the main game.

==Tips==

*Pointing straight above the basket with the ball has the best chance of making a basket.
*Standing directly under the basket and looking up through it, then spamming drop and pickup will allow the player to score repeatedly, as the score still goes up even if the ball goes through the basket from underneath.
**Setting your pickup and throw keybinds to the same key, allows the player to repeatedly press the button to easily get a high score.

==Trivia==

*When the scoreboard reaches 666 baskets, lightning will strike.
*Basketballs can also be found in some maps such as [[Brownstone High School]], though there is no similar hoop with counter to throw into.
