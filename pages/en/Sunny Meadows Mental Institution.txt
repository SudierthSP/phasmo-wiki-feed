}}'''Sunny Meadows Mental Institution''' (or simply '''Sunny Meadows''') is a [[map]] in ''Phasmophobia'' and the largest by number of rooms, succeeding the original [[Asylum]] map. It is a two-story decrepit [[wikipedia:psychiatric hospital|psychiatric hospital]] that can either be played as a whole map or separated into five '''Restricted''' areas, one of which is randomly selected for the contract.

==Description==
Sunny Meadows seems to once have been a Catholic parish/asylum as it features an old chapel and has several nods to it having once been a religious institution. Unlike most other maps, the building seems to be completely abandoned, and even somewhat in a hurry (for example, most of the "surgical" equipment in the morgue is still in place, as are some decaying corpses and human body parts). It is unclear what exactly made the asylum shut down and how long ago it happened.

It is a run-down, cluttered environment, with every footstep reverberating through the halls. Take note of the [[ghost]]'s scratching along the walls, and beware its wrath -- you may find yourself separated from your fellow investigators during a [[hunt]] as the wings' doors lock shut.

While the derelict stone environment and profane Chapel are eerie enough, the true horrors lie within the location's bowels: blood-stained walls, rotting corpses, and piles of gore provide ample indication of the atrocities performed within. The screams of the poor souls tortured and slain within its dark confines can still be heard through the quiet blackness.

==Structure==
Sunny Meadows contains 69 rooms, split into 5 wings and across 2 floors, as well as an outdoor courtyard. The wings are:
*'''Courtyard Wing:''' Built around a large courtyard, this wing contains amenities for patients and visitors as well as staff facilities.
*'''Male Wing:''' This wing contains several bedrooms, a dormitory, a common bathroom, as well as a day room for the male patients of the institution.
*'''Female Wing:''' This wing contains several bedrooms, a dormitory, a common bathroom, as well as a day room for the female patients of the institution.
*'''Hospital Wing:''' This wing contains several common hospital rooms as well as an operating room and a macabre morgue, still reeking with the remains of its past residents.
*'''Restricted Wing:''' This wing was presumably where the most aggressive or unfortunate patients received their "treatments" under the form of brutal torture, containing several cells filled with blood and gore.

===Layout tips===
*A labeled map, albeit one that is weathered and altogether difficult to read, is provided in the Entrance Corridor. 
**The room names '''Servant Room 1''' and '''Servant Room 2''' are swapped on this map from what is actually defined in-game.
*Certain rooms contain old bell-type alarm clocks and metronomes which the ghost might interact with.
*The crosses found hanging on the walls in various parts of the building can be turned upside down by the ghost, which will emit a loud scratching sound. Finding a cross that has been turned upside down (except for the ones in the Chapel) is a strong sign that the ghost is nearby. Crosses cannot be flipped back upright once they have been interacted with.

When a [[hunt]] begins, if the player is in the same wing as the ghost, the [[door]]s that allow to leave the wing will lock, forcing hunters to work with the area of the map that they find themselves in, potentially separating players from one-another. As such, it is wise to check for hiding spots both near where investigation is taking place and in wings on the route to the [[Ghost Room|favourite room]], as running to known hiding spots may not be possible. Beware, the ghost can still pass through these doors during a hunt.

When running away from a ghost, make good use of the various props such as dividers and cabinets cluttering the hallways in order to repeatedly break line of sight and reach a hiding spot or even throw it off, as more often than not the hiding spots are located at the ends and beginnings of hallways.

==Sunny Meadows Mental Institution - Restricted==
The restricted version of the map can be selected independently of the main location, and is considered a medium map. In the Restricted version, only the immediate entrance area (including the Chapel where the [[Cursed possession|Cursed Possession]]s spawn) and one randomly-selected wing of the building will be able to be accessed, with the entrances to the others being blocked off.

Bones spawn randomly, but will only spawn in areas that a ghost can also walk on, within a room that can be chosen as a [[Ghost Room|favourite room]]. Additionally, the bone cannot spawn in '''Manager's Office''' and '''Waiting Room'''.

==Cursed possession locations==
All the [[cursed possession]]s are located in the Chapel: the [[Monkey Paw]] at the foot of the large crucifix, and five other possessions arranged around the [[Summoning Circle]] in the middle.

==Apocalypse Challenge==
{{Main|Apocalypse Challenge}}
The Apocalypse Challenge can only be completed in Sunny Meadows. This is indicated with a golden skull on the map polaroid of the [[Lobby#Map selection board|map selection board]] when in Singleplayer.

==Trivia==
*A number of tags each listing a possible wish for the Monkey Paw can be found in various rooms.
*Ghosts can blow out some of the lit candles that are found in the Chapel. This may even result in an early [[Onryo]] hunt.
*Despite being classified as a large map, Sunny Meadows is actually smaller than both [[Prison]] and [[Brownstone High School]]—both originally Medium maps—by floor area within the map (investigation area).
*Sunny Meadows Restricted was originally a Small map, and was changed to Medium in 0.10.0, making it the second map to change size classes across versions of the game.
*Female Day Room, Male Day Room, as well as West Visitors' Room each have a standing lamp that needs to be turned on in addition to the main room lights for full sanity drain prevention.
*The stairs in Right Entrance Stairwell look to lead to a possible upper floor, however they are blocked up.

==Easter eggs==
*The map contains various elements which seems to be telling the story of one of the [[Ghost Model|ghost models]], semi-officially named ''Patient 07''.
**In [[0.6.0.1]], the "pajama girl" ghost model was changed to wear white gowns with a barcode and a label saying "Patient 07". In the same update, on the former [[Asylum]] map, one of the patient rooms had tally marks counting up to 1080 on its walls which could be revealed using a UV flashlight or Glowstick, as well as the following writing:<br><br>''I have to leave<br>Somewhere new<br>I don't belong here<br>I'll go soon<br>To get better, I hope<br>I found a new home<br>Sunny Meadows<br>Sounds nice, a place to heal''<br><br>
**In Padded Room 1 in the Hospital Wing, tally marks counting up to 7560 can be seen with a [[UV Flashlight]] or [[Glowstick]], as well as bloody handprints covering the door. This suggests that it might have been Patient 07's room once she was transferred to Sunny Meadows, or at least was locked in there for a significant period of time (approximately 20.7 years if the marks are counted as days).
**Finally, Patient 07's corpse can be found rotting in the Morgue. This means that Patient 07 died (most likely of poor treatment and brutal torture) in Sunny Meadows and that her tormented soul has since been condemned to haunt the world of the living.
*If the player lights the [[Summoning Circle]] in Custom difficulty with at least six cursed possessions in the game, every lit candle will turn a crucifix in the room upside down. If the last candle is lit, the large cross in the Chapel will ignite.
**The [[temperature]] of the ignited large cross is {{Temperature|666}} (the devil's number).
**This easter egg is hinted at by many writings on the Chapel's walls. "Six Becomes One" is written multiple times both using Latin and runic alphabets.
*A [[potato]] can be found in the Bake House, inside the 2nd oven from the left on the top row.
