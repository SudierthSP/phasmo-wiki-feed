}}'''0.7.1.0''', also known as the '''Halloween '22!''' update, is a minor update to the game released on 7 October 2022. This update introduces a Halloween-themed event, adds several quality-of-life changes, and fixes bugs.<ref>[https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/3328747612248417421 Halloween '22! | Update v0.7.1.0 (on Steam)]</ref>

== Additions ==
=== Trick or Treat ===
<blockquote>''Come and join us with a Halloween-themed event!''</blockquote>
* Players can obtain a Halloween Trophy for completing the [[Special events#Halloween 2022|event]]:
** Play on Nightmare [[difficulty]] without heavy rain
** Enter any [[map]] labelled with a pumpkin sticker on the contract selection board
** In order: collect candies and light jack o' lanterns, and take a [[photo]] of the [[ghost]]
** Do this on all labelled maps
* Event will be available until early November

=== Maps ===
;[[Sunny Meadows Mental Institution]]
* Added window-knocking sounds to windows in Sunny Meadows

=== Equipment ===
;[[Candle]]
* Lighting a candle will now produce sound

;Jenga Blocks
* Can now be placed
** Will temporarily still fall over upon loading into lobby

== Changes ==
=== Gameplay ===
*Lowered volume and length of some female ‘out of breath' sounds

==Bug fixes==
* Fixed several bugs with the global voice chat
* You can no longer hear dead players 
* The truck can now be closed if a player body did not despawn when they left
* The Halloween lobby easter egg will now work in VR
