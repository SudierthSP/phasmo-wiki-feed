}}'''0.8.1.4''', also known as the '''Easter Event '23''' update, is a minor update released on 5 April 2023.<ref>[https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/6770639657682498365 ''Easter Event '23 | Update v0.8.1.4''. 5 April 2023 (on Steam).]</ref> This update introduces an Easter event.

== Additions ==
=== [[Special events#Easter 2023|Easter '23]] ===
<blockquote>''Happy Easter everyone!''</blockquote>
* Players can obtain a badge and a trophy for completing the event:
** Enter any location featuring an Easter sticker on any difficulty in single or multi-player.
** Find and collect all of the hidden Easter eggs.
** Retreat to the truck and leave! No need to find the ghost type!
** Repeat this until all stickers on the contract board are ticked off!
* Available until 12 April 2023.
