The '''sound recorder''' is a device used to record [[Media#Sound evidence|sound evidence]].

==Mechanics==
The Sound Recorder can be {{Control|toggled}} on to begin scanning the area for any noise disturbance produced by the ghost. When it detects a valid noise, its sound meter will start to rise, indicating to the player how close they are to the source of the sound. To record a sound, the player must hold the {{Control|Use}} button to start recording and get sufficiently close to the source (indicated by the meter lights turning green on Tier I, the graph going above the bar on Tier II, and the circle at the center of the radar turning solid on Tier III). The recording will stop automatically once a valid sound is captured. If a sound is considered as a Duplicate, the Tier I's lights will be less bright and the Tier II and III's sound waves will be faded.

Up to 3 audio clips can be stored in the [[journal]] per [[contract]]. The recorder picks up ghost-related sounds such as [[Paranormal Sound|paranormal sounds]] or [[ghost event]]s, as well as evidence-related sounds such as [[Spirit Box]] responses, [[Ghost Writing]], or [[EMF Level 5]]. The quality of the recording depends on the proximity to the sound and the tier of the recorder.

The Tier III Sound Recorder has directional audio and visual indicators that can be helpful in locating the source of the sound.

===Ghost-related mechanics===
*The [[Banshee]] has a 33% chance to produce one of its unique shrieks instead of a regular paranormal sound.
*The [[Moroi]] can curse a player who tries to record a sound.
*The [[Myling]] produces paranormal sounds on the sound recorder more frequently.

==Tips==
*Sound Recorders (especially the Tier III) can be used to locate the ghost by following the sound levels it picks up, much like the [[EMF Reader|EMF reader]] with EMF signatures. Due to their relatively short range however, they are best used on smaller maps.
