|description=An infrared motion sensor that detects both human and paranormal movement, when set off it will illuminate the surrounding area with a bright light.}}The '''Infrared Light Sensor''' was an item that helped to indicate the ghost's physical location. It was removed in [[0.3.0]]. 
==Usage==
When a player, dead player or ghost walked past the infrared light sensor, it would illuminate the surrounding area with a bright light, allowing players to track the ghost to a certain degree while on-site. The Infrared Light Sensor did not provide feedback to the [[truck]], nor did it count for the "Detect the ghost with a motion sensor" [[Objectives|objective]].

Due to its very limited utility, it was merged with the [[Motion Sensor]].
