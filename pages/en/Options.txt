== Video ==
{| class="fandom-table"
!Option
!Description
!Default
|-
|Brightness
|Sets the brightness and contrast of the UI and objects.
|100%
|-
|Fullscreen Mode
|Sets the game to fullscreen.<br>Doesn't show in VR.
|Fullscreen
|-
|VSync
|Limits the game's framerate to the computer refresh rate.<br>Doesn't show in VR.
|On
|-
|Resolution
|Sets the fullscreen game resolution and framerate.<br>Doesn't show in VR.
|''Depends on device capabilities''
|-
|Anti-Aliasing
|Smoothens and eliminates jagged lines on the edges of objects, improving graphical quality at the expense of performance.
|Off
|-
|SMAA Quality
|Sets the quality of the SMAA.<br>Only shows when SMAA is selected for Anti-Aliasing.<br>Doesn't show in VR.
|Low
|-
|MSAA Quality
|Sets the quality of the MSAA.<br>Only shows when MSAA is selected for Anti-Aliasing.
|Off
|-
|FidelityFX Super Resolution 1.0
|Renders the game at a lower resolution, then uses artificial intelligence to upscale it to a higher resolution, improving performance at the expense of quality. May not work for older graphics cards.
|Off
|-
|Override Sharpness
|Increases sharpness when using FidelityFX.<br>Only shows when FidelityFX is enabled.
|?
|-
|Sharpness Amount
|The amount of sharpness applied.<br>Only shows when Override Sharpness is enabled.
|?
|-
|Texture Resolution
|Sets the quality of object textures.
|Full
|-
|Skylight
|Whether the sun and moon casts light on objects.
|On
|-
|Fog Quality
|Sets the quality of fog effects during foggy [[weather]].
|Medium
|-
|Shadows
|Whether shadow edges are sharp or graduated.
|Soft
|-
|Shadow Resolution
|Sets the quality of shadows.
|Medium
|-
|Anisotropic
|Sharperns the look of objects that are further away.
|On
|-
|Ambient Occlusion
|Improves the realism of shadows and lighting.
|On
|-
|Bloom
|Creates a strong glow effect around light sources.
|Off
|-
|Film Grain
|Creates a grainy effect on the player's screen.<br>Doesn't show in VR.
|On
|-
|Chromatic Aberration
|Creates a blue-red effect around edges of objects.<br>Doesn't show in VR.
|On
|-
|Eye Adaption
|Simulates the eyes adjusting to light or dark areas over time.<br>Doesn't show in VR.
|Off
|-
|Screen Space Illumination
|Simulates bounced lighting, making lighting and shadows look less dark.<br>Doesn't show in VR.
|Off
