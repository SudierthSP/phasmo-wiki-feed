'''Mannequins''' are props that the [[ghost]] can interact with. They are most commonly found in garages and basements.

==Mechanics==
Players cannot interact with or move mannequins in any way. However, they can block the ghost's line-of-sight during [[hunt]]s. When a ghost [[Interaction|interacts]] with mannequins, it will leave an [[EMF]] 2 reading (33% chance for [[EMF Level 5]] instead for applicable ghosts) at its base. If the mannequin gets teleported, the EMF reading will be located at the mannequin's original location.

;Dress Form
Dress forms can either be rotated or teleported elsewhere at fixed spots by the ghost.

;Mannequin
Similarly to the dress form, the ghost can either change the mannequin's pose (51% chance), rotate it (19% chance), or teleport it elsewhere (30% chance), usually near the player, when interacting with it.

The ghost cannot interact with mannequins while a player is looking at them.
