The '''video camera''' is a piece of electronic [[Equipment#Starter Equipment|Starter Equipment]] used to detect [[Ghost Orb]]s and to record videos.

==Mechanics==
When a video camera is {{Control|picked up}}, the flip screen will open automatically. The video camera can be used handheld with its built-in display or {{Control|placed}} on a flat surface or a [[tripod]]. The video camera will automatically start streaming its [[Video Feed|video feed]] to the [[computer]] when held outside the [[truck]] or placed.

Holding the placement key will show a translucent preview of the video camera and its facing direction, which can be adjusted while holding the placement key by moving the mouse left and right. Be aware that even though certain spots are shown as valid, the camera may tip over or fall due to the geometry or other objects placed on the surface.

When the ghost manifests during a [[ghost event]] or a [[hunt]], it will cause interference on all nearby video cameras and distort the image on both the built-in display and the video feed. Cameras of higher Tier with a better image quality will be significantly less impacted by ghost interference, aiding players in observing or tracking the ghost during a ghost event or a hunt.

Ghosts may occasionally knock over placed down video cameras within a certain range, forcing players to actively tend to them. Placing a camera on a tripod will reduce the probability of the ghost knocking it over. The stability provided by the tripod will depend on its Tier.

Night vision can be {{Control|toggled}} while holding the active video camera or via the computer keyboard. When night vision is turned on, any Ghost Orbs present can be seen through the video camera.

Holding the {{Control|Use}} button while holding the active video camera will start a video recording. If a valid subject is captured, it will be recorded in the [[Media]] tab in the [[journal]]. Video recording can also be started from the [[computer]] in the [[truck]] by clicking on the Rec button. If a subject is considered as a Duplicate, the highlight frame's text will be faded.

==Tips==
*The video camera's night vision function can be very useful when using a [[D.O.T.S Projector]] as the [[ghost]]'s silhouette may be easier to see with night vision.
*The video camera can also be used to maintain surveillance on areas such as hallways, rooms, and the [[fuse box]]. This can allow for easy viewing of certain stationary evidence like [[Fingerprints]], [[Ghost Writing]], [[EMF Level 5]], [[salt]], or [[D.O.T.S Projector]]. However, the video feed becomes extremely distorted whenever the [[ghost]] manifests during a [[Ghost Event]] or a [[hunt]], making observation very difficult.
**Note that if the camera isn't positioned properly and/or is of a lower tier which has worse quality, it can be difficult to spot Ghost Writing and read EMF Level 5 off the EMF Reader; Players should still go in personally and check evidence every now and then to prevent missing any.
*It is recommended to turn your flashlight (and room lights) off while using night vision to check for [[Ghost Orb|Ghost Orbs]], as it makes the camera feed extremely bright, making it nearly impossible to see through the camera to spot any Orbs.
