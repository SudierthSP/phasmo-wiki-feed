}}'''0.3.1.2''' is a minor update to the game released on 29 September 2021,<ref>https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/2859213830222433359</ref> with a same-version hotfix released the next day.<ref>https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/2859213830226061453</ref> The update mainly fixes bugs.

== Changes ==
=== Gameplay ===
*The anniversary decorations have been removed and the music on the boom box will now default to off
*Updated a lot of missing localisation for Turkish

== Bug Fixes ==
=== From versions released before 0.3.1.2 ===
*Fixed an exploit where you could point lights up at ceilings to prevent your sanity draining
*Fixed a bug where the journal case number was being increased by the amount of players in the game

=== From 0.3.1.2 (as fixed in hotfix) ===
*Fixed a bug where you would crash if you got kicked
*Fixed a bug where the video cameras screen would turn green if they were placed on the belt
*Fixed a bug where DOTS projectors and glow sticks would affect sanity drain
*Fixed an issue where flashlight would roll away when thrown
