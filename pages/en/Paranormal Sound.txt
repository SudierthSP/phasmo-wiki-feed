'''Paranormal sounds''' are normally inaudible sounds occasionally emitted by the [[ghost]].

==Mechanics==
Upon loading into the [[truck]] at the start of a [[contract]], the game will start a timer with a random duration between 80 and 127 seconds, at the end of which a paranormal sound will be created at the approximate location of the ghost. A new timer will then start.

When a paranormal sound is created, the [[Sound Recorder|sound recorder]] has a time frame of 15 seconds plus the duration of the sound to record it. The [[Parabolic Microphone|parabolic microphone]] will pick up the sound 15 seconds after it has been created or when the player is actively hearing it through the sound recorder. However, if the player records the sound with a sound recorder, they will be unable to hear it again through the parabolic microphone.

The emitted sound itself is randomly chosen among a list of possible sounds depending on the ghost's gender.

===Ghost-specific abilities===
*The [[Banshee]] has a 33% chance to replace the paranormal sound with one of its unique screams.
*The [[Moroi]] curses players who hear any paranormal sound through the parabolic microphone or record one with the sound recorder.
*The [[Myling]] has a timer with a random duration between 64 and 127 seconds instead of 80 and 127 seconds.
