=== "Low to ground strategy" ===
The low to ground strategy is a maneuver that can be performed by VR [[player]]s in an attempt to avoid being killed during a [[hunt]]. It was fixed in the 0.26 update on 6 March 2021. A similar bug reoccurred in the [[VR Overhaul Update]].

To perform this maneuver, the player had to lie on their stomach or back as low to the ground as possible. A [[ghost]] successfully kills their target player when hunting by colliding with them, but reducing the character's size also reduces the chance of collision. In this case, the character's hitbox (an arbitrary shape that the game uses to determine whether the player is colliding with things) shortens and possibly descends into the floor, meaning that the ghost cannot touch it, and thus could not kill the player.

Doing this could help the player get photographic evidence of the ghost by pointing a photo camera upwards and taking pictures, without risk of death.

Youtuber ''hudzell'' released a [https://youtu.be/K3j5FQ7R-N4 video] demonstrating this strategy in October 2020, adding during the video that players might not need to crouch too low to perform it.

Players using this trick would find their vision possibly restricted from being prone (unless they lie on their back), and they may also find the experience physically uncomfortable. Also, moving too close to some objects may cause the player to go out of bounds, teleporting them back outside the house or in the truck.<ref>[https://youtu.be/K3j5FQ7R-N4?t=455 ''Tanglewood Street House | VR | Professional SOLO Demon | Phasmophobia'' | hudzell | YouTube].</ref>

Obviously, Non-VR players cannot use this strategy, as they do not have the ability to lay down.

== Notorious bugs ==
Below is a list of patched glitches that were either peculiarly game-breaking and/or gained significant notoriety in the community.

=== Door physics ===
In the past, door physics were notoriously janky and prone to many glitches. The most common was the ability to push into doors and tear them off their hinges into awkward angles. This did not, however, "jam" doors or prevent them from being closed/locked by the ghost, as they would reset to their normal position as soon as they were interacted with again. Door physics could also be exploited to traverse walls or cieilings, allowing players to go out of bounds or exit the investigation area during a hunt. For a short time after the early access Steam release, kitchen cupboard doors shared the same physics properties as passageway doors, allowing for certain exploits such as climbing on countertops, out of the ghost's reach. 

=== "Show us" ===
During a brief period, saying the phrase "show us" would always force the ghost to perform an interaction no matter what. A widely-exploited side effect of this glitch was that it would immediately cancel ongoing hunts. This glitch became widely notorious at the time for its absurd power. 

=== Hantu super speed ===
In version [[0.7.1.4]], a glitch was introduced causing the Hantu's ability of accelerating in cold rooms to increase the speed value exponentially. This led to astonishingly high speeds up to approximately 19 m/s, breaking the ghost's pathfinding and making hunts absurdly difficult to survive.

== Not bugs ==
The following behaviours, although they may seem unexpected, are features working as designed/won't be fixed:
* Maps are an ugly red and black when eye adaption is turned on, caused by an incompatible graphics card
* Losing consumable items even when not dying (such as [[salt]] and [[Sanity Medication|sanity medication]])
* Seeing freezing breath with non-freezing ghosts. Since [[0.9.0]], freezing breath occurs whenever temperatures drop below 5°C (41°F).
* The Goryo changing favorite room after using the "I wish for sanity" wish on the Monkey Paw.
* UV flashlights not working when flashlights are disabled in the custom difficulty settings.
* Summoning Circle candles extinguishing right after lighting them when at 0% sanity.
* Crucifixes being used while inside the truck. Although very unexpected (usually occuring on [[42 Edgefield Road]] due to the proximity of the truck to the house), this is simply due to the range-based functionality of the crucifix.
* Labeled photos with no visible subject in the frame. Photos are intentionally not synched over with other players for performance reasons, therefore it is possible that photos taken by other players may appear incongruent in the journal.
* Not being able to take photos of some interactions like opened doors or thrown objects. Since [[0.13.0]], photos of certain subjects have been removed, and must now instead be recorded using a [[Video Camera]].
