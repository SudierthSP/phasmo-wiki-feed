}}'''0.7.0''', also known as the '''Apocalypse''' update, was released on 27 September 2022.<ref>[https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/3360267827388792766 ''Apocalypse | Major Update v0.7.0.0'' (on Steam)]</ref><ref group="note">Content Creators on the official Phasmophobia Discord were given early access keys on 26 September 2022.</ref> The update includes options to customise game difficulty, a replacement of the existing [[Asylum]] map, a new small variant of the existing [[Maple Lodge Campsite]] map, an overhaul to the lobby/main menu UI, a new player reporting system, changes to several existing ghosts, and others.

== Additions and Changes ==

=== Maps ===
;General
* Redesigned the [[fuse box]] for house and farmhouse maps
* Increased rewards for playing on larger maps
* Renamed some maps

;[[Camp Woodwind]]
*New map, small variant of [[Maple Lodge Campsite]] (the latter will be kept)

;[[Grafton Farmhouse]]
*Added a house [[candle]]

;[[Maple Lodge Campsite]]
* Trees next to cabin now block line-of-sight
* Ghosts can no longer set certain outdoor paths as [[ghost room]]s
* Rebaked occlusion to improve performance
* Adjusted campfire particles and bloom

;[[Sunny Meadows Mental Institution]]
*New map to replace Asylum<ref>[https://discord.com/channels/435431947963990026/765596827537244200/973908282387357737 ''"Asylum will be removed when Sunny Meadows is added"'' cjdxn, ''Phasmophobia'' Lead Artist (on Discord)]</ref>
*Playable as a "Restricted" variant with four of five sections blocked off

;[[Willow Street House]]
*Added new props in memory of Lead Artist cjdxn's cat, Willow

=== Gameplay ===
;General
* Main menu revamp with additional UI features (such as ID-card based stats system) to be introduced gradually in the upcoming updates
* New look for the player [[Lobby]], with equipement shop and buyout revamps
* New collectibles and interactables
* New ambience sounds
* Adjusted skyboxes for each weather type
* Improved heavy rain and snow performance
* Dead players can now see doors
* Reduced the volume of the guitar and camping stove

;[[Lobby]] and [[main menu board|main menu]]
* Replaced the existing lobby with a new lobby
* Includes new interfaces split into a shop, investigation boards, access to the truck, and a staff room
* Added player ID cards
* Added a "Detailed Statistics" panel to the post-game screen
* Added long-term statistics tracking to the journal for various player and ghost statistics
* Players will now connect to the last-joined region instead of the region with the best ping
* Journal can now be used in lobby
* Steam users can now view and add friends via the new "Recent players" journal tab
* Added shortcut buttons to jump between Location Selection, Main Menu, and Shop screens
** Arrow keys can also be used for navigation
* Added new collectible cabinets to the lobby
** Each cursed possession will be added the first time it is used
** Collecting each type of bone will add it to the collection (some need to be added twice)
** More collectibles to be added in later updates
* Added the option to create loadouts
** Additional loadouts will be added in the Progression update
* Up to 10 photos can now be viewed in lobby, instead of 6

;[[Objectives]] and [[Daily and Weekly Tasks|challenges]]
* Added weekly challenges
** More to be added in the Progression update
* Increased rewards for all daily challenges

;[[Media|Photo Rewards]]
*Added new rewardable items:
** D.O.T.S. Ghost
** Used crucifix (once or twice)
** Salt pile when stepped in
** Ghost Writing will now be labelled as Ghost Writing instead of Interaction
* Collecting the [[Bone Evidence|bone]] will now reward more on larger maps
* Removed the photo "point" system
** Each photo will now reward a set amount of money and XP
* Photos are no longer affected by the difficulty multiplier
* Improved the photo priority system
** Scored by distance and how close they are to the middle of the photo
** Ghost will always take priority in all cases
** Improved reliability

* Replaced the whiteboard in the [[truck]] with a progressively updating investigation board
* Moved the key next to the keypad

=== Ghosts ===
;General
* All hunting, [[ghost event]], and [[Music Box]] singing sound volumes now drop to 0% when the player is more than 20 metres away
* Increased the throw power range from 0.1 - 2.5 to 1 - 3
* Objects are now thrown higher
** Results in more consistent throws
* Increased the chance of item throws during hunts
** 50% chance every 0.5 seconds for most ghosts
** 100% chance every 0.5 seconds for [[Poltergeist]]
* Will now use sprinting sound effect when hunting on dirt floors

;[[Demon]]
* Any-sanity hunt will now happen more reliably

;[[Jinn]]
* Ability will now apply if the player is in the same room as the ghost, or within 3 metres
* The EMF will now be generated at the fuse box when anyone's sanity is drained, instead of just the host

;[[Oni]]
* Now drains sanity at double the standard rate during ghost events
* Increased the length range of it flashing visible during hunts

;[[Poltergeist]]
* Oni throw power transferred to Poltergeist
** Increased throw power from 0 - 5 (for Oni) to 2 - 6

=== Equipment ===
;General
* All equipment can now be placed back on truck wall
* Reworked the placement highlight system
** Highlight will now show only when holding down placement key (defaults to F on PC and Trigger on VR)
** Moving the mouse left/right while holding the placement key will now rotate the highlighted object
** Release to place item
** Adjusted placeable areas of various equipments

;[[Candle]]
* Will now extinguish when placed into inventory

;[[Crucifix]]
* A successful block of a hunt attempt will now create an EMF 2 reading

;[[Ouija Board]]
* Sanity drain for "Hide and seek" reduced from 25% to 0%

;[[Sound Sensor]]
* Will now show room name of the room it is physically in, instead of the room the player was standing in when they placed it

;[[Tripod]]
* Can now be rotated and placed

=== Player reporting system ===
* Players can now report other uncivil players through a new reporting system
* Bans are progressive and increase in duration for multiple offences (some offences may start further in the chain than others): 2 days/7 days/30 days/permanent
* Misuse of the reporting system may also result in bans
* Report a player by opening the new "Recent players" tab in the journal and clicking the report icon next to that players name
* Banned players may still play singleplayer
* Reduced RAM usage for fonts
* Adjusted several light bloom values

=== Controls ===
* Improved consistency for equipment usage
** Placeable objects will always use the secondary button (defaults to F)
** Usable objects will always use the primary button (defaults to Right Mouse)

=== General ===
* Added text and voice recognition support for Arabic
* VR players will now see a loading screen to help with loading jitters
* Save files will now create a backup and automatically restore if they corrupt
* Objects will no longer render behind closed doors
* Game options can now only be viewed in journal
* Added screen-spaced reflections to journal options
* Changed default journal button on a gamepad to "select" instead of D-Pad up

== Bug fixes ==
* Fixed long-distance roams:
** High - 30%
** Medium - 20%
** Low - 10%
* Adjusted the chance of ghosts changing its favourite room
** If a ghost long roams into a new room, do a chance check:
** Low - 33%
** Medium - 66%
** High - 100%
** Then perform check based on sanity (e.g. 30% sanity = 70% chance)
** If successful for both chances, change favourite room
* Goryo will now have a half the chance of other ghosts to long-roam instead of double
* Myling will now decrease the audible range of its hunt audio, instead of only footsteps
* If you have less than 16% sanity when lighting the last Summoning Circle, the ghost will now hunt instantly after being summoned
* When the summoning circle is lit, the ghost's position will now sync faster for non-hosts
* Fingerprints on camping lamps will now be the correct size
* You can no longer place objects on the truck ceiling
* Fixed a rare issue where the voice recognition would not work due to an empty voice models file
* Picking up a tripod with a camcorder attached by someone else, will no longer break the camcorder
* You are no longer unkillable when hiding under stairs or similar objects in some locations
* The hiding spot next to the cabin will no longer get you killed
* Grafton's upstairs locker can now be blocked
* Bleasdale's workshop locker can now be blocked
* Having ALT as your push-to-talk will no longer keep your voice active after alt-tabbing
* The toilets upstairs in Edgefield now flush
* Ghosts will no longer affect the living room collider when standing on the basement stairs in Tanglewood
* You can no longer glitch behind the small tents to access safe spots
* The Mare will no longer stop attempting to do its light switch ability after you use the light switch several times
* Doors will no longer spin when closing for a hunt
