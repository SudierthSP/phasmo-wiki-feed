}}'''10 Ridgeview Court''' (or simply '''Ridgeview''') is a small [[map]] in ''Phasmophobia''. A two-story, four-bedroom house with a garage and basement, Ridgeview presents a spacious living area, giving way to more congested linear hallways. However, the presence of closets and multiple sub-rooms to dive into provides plenty of safety during a [[hunt]].

==Structure==
Ridgeview has four bedrooms, four toilets, a garage and a basement, among other rooms.

===Layout tips===
*The "Master Bedroom" consists of the bedroom itself, the attached bathroom, and the closet that can be accessed through said bathroom.
*The small hallway leading to "Master Bedroom" is considered part of "Kitchen".
*The Upstairs Hallway includes both the main hallway that turns a corner and leads to "Boys Bedroom" and "Bathroom" and the straight hallway partially separated by a wall that leads to "Teen Boys Bedroom", "Girls Bedroom", and "Upstairs Toilet".
*In order to fully prevent passive sanity drain in the Foyer, the light switch that turns on the lamp outside at the entrance door has to be turned on as well.
*There is a bug where the computer in Hallway needs to be turned on to prevent passive sanity drain in that room.

The following rooms allow short [[Roaming|roams]] to another floor to be completed:
*Basement

Several more unofficial furniture-based hiding spots are available such as:
*'''Kitchen''', behind the fridge
*'''Hallway''', behind the piano or the computer

[[Guides/Surviving a hunt#Looping the ghost|Looping]] the ghost may be performed around the dining table. The ghost can also be looped around multiple shelves in the Basement (the collection of shelves on the right side opposite one of the [[Fuse Box]] locations is the easiest spot).

==Bone locations==
Bones spawn randomly, but will only spawn in areas that a ghost can also walk on. Additionally, the bone cannot spawn in '''Foyer''' and '''Upstairs Hallway'''.
