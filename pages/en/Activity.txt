# 源: https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/api.php'''Activity''' is a mechanic affecting how the [[ghost]] will interact with its environment, making it [[Interaction|move objects]], [[Roaming|roam]], use abilities, etc.

==Mechanics==
===Activity calculation===
By default, the [[ghost]] starts with an activity level of 0 at 100% average [[sanity]]. The activity increases when the ''insanity'' increases. In other words, for each % of sanity lost, the activity increases by 1.

Playing solo will increase the activity by 15.

When the ghost is affected by [[Incense]], activity will increase by a random number between 20 and 29. This number decreases by 0.5 every second.

Some ghosts have additional modifiers to the activity:
*If a player is within 6 metres or in the same room as the [[Oni]], activity will increase by 30.
*If a player uses [[voice chat]] in the same room as the [[Yokai]], activity will increase by 30.
*At its youngest, the [[Thaye]]'s activity is multiplied by 2. This multiplier decreases each time it ages to a minimum of 0.5 at its oldest.
*The [[Obambo]]'s activity is fixed at 90 in its calm state, and at 25 in its aggressive state.

The activity level is capped at 100.

===Actions===
When the ghost fails the chance to [[hunt]] after exiting an idle state, it will perform an activity check based on the [[difficulty]]'s threshold:

<br>If the [[weather]] is Blood Moon, the threshold will be decreased by 15. If the ghost is a [[Shade]], the threshold will be further multiplied by 1.5 and rounded down to the nearest integer.

The check has a 50% chance to succeed if the activity calculated above is greater than or equal to a random number between 0 and the threshold minus 1. The chance to succeed is increased to 100% if ''The Tower'' [[Tarot Cards|tarot card]] has been drawn in the last 20 seconds or if the [[Monkey Paw]]'s 'activity wish' has been used in the last 2 minutes.

If the check succeeds, the ghost will choose one of the following actions:
*45.45% (5/11) chance to perform an [[interaction]]
*36.36% (4/11) chance to enter an [[ability]] state, which makes it either interact with the [[Fuse Box|fuse box]], use its ability, or perform a [[Ghost Event|ghost event]]
*18.18% (2/11) chance to enter a [[roaming]] state

If the check fails, the ghost will choose one of the following actions:
*66.67% (2/3) chance to either enter a [[Ghost Room|favourite room]] state (75% chance) or perform an interaction (25% chance)
**83.33% (5/6) chance instead if the ghost is a [[Goryo]]
*33.33% (1/3) chance to enter a roaming state
**16.67% (1/6) chance instead if the ghost is a Goryo
