The '''Objective Board''' can be found in the [[truck]], between the [[computer]] and the [[Site Activity Monitor]] and [[Sound Monitor]].

==Usage==
When in a [[contract]], the objective board will display the following information:
*The case number
*The ghost's name
*Whether the ghost responds to everyone or only people that are alone (this information is not provided on Professional, Nightmare, and Insanity [[difficulty|difficulties]])
*3 [[objectives]]
*5 video slots, 5 photo slots, and 3 sound slots that can be filled

==Trivia==
*Before [[0.174]], completed tasks were crossed out, instead of greyed out.
