}}'''0.5.1.0''' is a minor update to the game released on 24 December 2021.<ref>[https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/3099038120462683272 Cursed Possessions | Update v0.5.1.0]</ref> The update includes several changes and bug fixes.

== Changes ==
=== Equipment ===
;[[Candle]]
*Smoke particles have been adjusted

;[[Cursed possession]]s
*Will now only have one spawn point per item, with increased intuitiveness
**Will be placed in first room for [[Prison]], [[Brownstone High School]] and [[Asylum]]

;[[Tarot Cards]]
*Now easier to use in VR

=== Gameplay ===
;[[Weather]]
*Foggy weather has been temporarily removed
**Will be re-added in a future update

=== Ghosts ===
;General
*Maximum wander distance range has been increased slightly to prevent ghosts from being trapped in larger rooms

;[[Demon]]
*Description has been updated to better reflect its weakness

;[[Hantu]]
*Will now always have [[Freezing Temperatures]] evidence guaranteed on Nightmare difficulty
*Breath can no longer be seen by dead players

;[[Yurei]]
*"Silent ghost event" ability will now only close one door, instead of all of the doors near the ghost
*Will now stay in their room more reliably when smudged

=== Maps ===
;[[Prison]]
*Light control computers now change their colour to green when lights are on, and red when off

;[[Asylum]]
*Reduced the room count shown on the contract selection screen

=== General ===
*Updated to the latest LIV SDK to fix a VR crash
