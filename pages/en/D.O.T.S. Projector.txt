}}The '''D.O.T.S. Projector''' is a piece of electronic [[Equipment#Starter Equipment|starter equipment]], as well as a type of [[evidence]], that can be used to reveal the ghost's movement.

==Usage==
When activated, the D.O.T.S. Projector will emit a matrix of bright green laser points in a small area from the projector.

Though the D.O.T.S. Projector is an electronic equipment, only the Tier I D.O.T.S. Projector when activated and held will attract the ghost to the player during a [[hunt]]; Tiers II and III D.O.T.S. Projectors are always turned off when held.

===Tier I===
The Tier I D.O.T.S. Projector is a small flashlight, which range is a 7.5-metre-long cone with an angle of 30°. It can be {{Control|toggled}}, similarly to [[flashlight]]s, and can be {{Control|placed}}, similarly to [[Video Camera|video cameras]].

It cannot be turned on if the "Flashlights" [[Difficulty#Custom|custom difficulty]] setting is Off.

===Tier II===
The Tier II D.O.T.S. Projector is a projector that emits the D.O.T.S. field as a sphere with a radius of 2.4 metres rather than as a cone. It is automatically turned on when placed on a wall, a ceiling, or the floor, similarly to [[Motion Sensor|motion sensors]].

===Tier III===
Similarly to the Tier II, the Tier III D.O.T.S. Projector can be placed on various surfaces. When turned on, its range is a 7-metre-long cone with an angle of 54°, and it will slowly rotate to scan the environment. The rotation can be paused by {{Control|interacting}} with the projector. Interacting again will turn it off, and interacting a third time will turn it back on again and restart the rotation.

==Tips==
*If the favourite room is small, consider placing the D.O.T.S. Projector in a neighbouring room to aid visibility.
*The Projector emits a substantial amount of light when placed. Investigators can use them for illumination, especially with a spare Projector or when D.O.T.S. evidence can be safely crossed off. Note that the D.O.T.S. Projector light does not mitigate sanity drain.
*The green light emitted may obscure nearby [[Ultraviolet#Fingerprints|fingerprints]] or [[Ultraviolet#Footprints|footprints]]; look carefully or remove the projector when checking for ultraviolet evidence.

==Trivia==
*D.O.T.S. stands for "Detector Of The Supernatural".
*The D.O.T.S. Projector is inspired by the XBOX Kinect scene in the film ''Paranormal Activity 4''.<ref>[https://discord.com/channels/435431947963990026/754891077374443592/932065910800400445 ''"I came up with DOTS based on the XBOX kinect scene from Paranormal activity film"''. cjdxn, Lead Artist (on Discord). 16 January 2022.]</ref>
