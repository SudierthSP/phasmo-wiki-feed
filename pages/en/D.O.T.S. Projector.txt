}}The '''D.O.T.S Projector''' is a piece of electronic [[Equipment#Starter Equipment|starter equipment]], as well as a type of [[evidence]], that can be used to reveal the ghost's movement.

==Usage==
The D.O.T.S Projector can be {{Control|placed}} on a wall or floor similar to a [[Motion Sensor|motion sensor]]. It emits a matrix of bright green laser points in a small area from the projector. Tier I D.O.T.S Projector additionally can be held and {{Control|toggled}} to create a cone of D.O.T.S light where it is pointed. The Tier I D.O.T.S Projector will not work if [[Flashlight]]s are turned off in [[Difficulty#Custom|custom difficulty]] settings.

Though the D.O.T.S Projector is an electronic equipment, only the Tier I D.O.T.S. Projector when activated and held will attract the ghost to the player during a [[hunt]], and they are otherwise safe to hold onto.

==Tips==
*If the favourite room is small, consider placing the D.O.T.S Projector in a neighbouring room to aid visibility.
*The Projector emits a substantial amount of light when placed. Investigators can use them for illumination, especially with a spare Projector or when D.O.T.S evidence can be safely crossed off. Note that the D.O.T.S projector light does not mitigate sanity drain.
*The Tier II and III D.O.T.S Projectors can be placed on some ceilings in a few maps, which may allow the light to cover a room with much more consistency. This is especially useful for the tier III projector, whose sweeping motions and cone-shaped light area can cause the ghost to potentially leave its light prematurely depending on the ghost's and the projector's movement.
*The green light emitted may obscure nearby [[Ultraviolet#Fingerprints|fingerprints]] or [[Ultraviolet#Footprints|footprints]]; look carefully or remove the projector when checking for ultraviolet evidence.
*The tier III D.O.T.S Projector emits a constant motor whirring sound when active, which can both be quite annoying and interfere with the player's hearing when trying to listen for ghost activity. If you press {{Control|Interact}} when pointing at the projector, it will ''stop'' in its current position, allowing it to operate in complete silence at the cost of covering a smaller area; interacting again will turn it off completely, and interacting a third time will turn it back on again.

==Trivia==

*The D.O.T.S Projector is inspired by the XBOX Kinect scene in the film ''Paranormal Activity 4''.<ref>[https://discord.com/channels/435431947963990026/754891077374443592/932065910800400445 ''"I came up with DOTS based on the XBOX kinect scene from Paranormal activity film"''. cjdxn, Lead Artist (on Discord). 16 January 2022.]</ref>
