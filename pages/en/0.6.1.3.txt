}}The '''0.6.1.3''' update is a minor update to the game released on 15 April 2022.<ref>https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/5909294533687862799</ref> This update includes a time-limited event for Easter and fixes bugs. A same-version hotfix was released to fix some eggs being unobtainable.

== Additions ==
;Easter egg hunt
*Easter eggs hidden on each map
*Number of eggs left to find displayed on [[Site Map]]
*Finding all eggs will restore team sanity to 100%
*Ends on 19 April

;General
*New visual indicator for PC (bottom left) and VR (belt) when using toggle voice

== Changes ==
=== Map adjustments ===
;[[Prison]]
*Brightness of CCTV night vision increased

;General
*VR hold positions for all equipment have been refined, to make the hand model line up with controller

== Bug fixes ==
=== From versions released before 0.6.1.3 ===
*Fixed a bug where the CCTV camera number wasn’t updating in the truck
*Fixed a bug where Banshee’s were killing non-targets near doors
*Fixed a bug where hidden Willow hiding spots would still occlude the level
*Fixed a bug where a Ridgeview basement light didn’t turn on
*Fixed a bug where the ghost couldn’t interact with one of the paintings
*Fixed a bug where the top daily challenge slider was interactable
*VR:
**Fixed a bug where the Voodoo Doll’s left-hand rotation was wrong
**Fixed a bug where you were unable to light house candles
**Potential fix for the VIVE Cosmos mute button not working

=== From 0.6.1.3 (as fixed in hotfix) ===
*Fixed several eggs being uncollectable
