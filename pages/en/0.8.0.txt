}}'''0.8.0''', also known as the '''Tempest''' update, is a major update released on 14 December 2022.<ref>[https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/3651886751537076286 ''Tempest | Major Update v0.8.0.0'', 12 December 2022 (on Steam).]</ref><ref group="note">Content Creators on the official Phasmophobia Discord were given early access keys on 13 December 2022.</ref> The update includes an upgrade to the Universal Render Pipeline (URP), improvements to weather, changes to several existing ghosts, and others.

== Additions and Changes ==
=== [[Special events#Holiday 2022|Holiday '22]] ===
* New Phasmophobia 2022 Christmas event
* Earn a unique holiday trophy and an exclusive Holiday '22 ID card and badge

=== Equipment ===
;[[Candle]]
* Improved wax effects when lit

;[[Crucifix]]
* Can now be placed
* Effective range is now visible while being placed

;[[Haunted Mirror]]
* Improved effects and visibility

;[[Ouija Board]]
* All players must now be further than 5m from the Ouija Board before it breaks, instead of just the person who activated it
* Will now deactivate if the player walks outside the map

;[[Sanity Pills]]
* No longer disappear and now drop on the floor instead

;[[Smudge Sticks]]
* Improved burn effect

=== Gameplay ===
;Custom Difficulty
* Reduced rewards from custom difficulties by 80%
** Current and future challenges will still be accessible

;Investigation bonuses
* New bonuses for completing objectives in [[contract]]s
** '''Investigation bonus:''' doubled money and experience for secondary [[objectives]] if the player identifies the ghost correctly and escapes alive
** '''Perfect investigation bonus:''' In addition to the above, collect the bone and fill the journal with 3-star photos, to gain a further bonus on top of the investigation bonus
** Investigation bonuses are affected by the difficulty multiplier
* Added new Payments page to [[journal]]
* Reduced secondary objective rewards to accomodate investigation bonus changes

;[[Objectives]]
* Removed salt objective

;Weather
* Re-added foggy weather
** Will now only show where it makes sense
* Added new Sunrise weather
** Locations will start with temperatures between {{Temperature|10|18}}
* Improved weather effects and visuals

=== Ghosts ===
;General
* Ghost flickering is now synced across players
* Ghosts now affect lights at the same range as equipment on the same floor

;[[Goryo]]
* Will not longer change favourite room or roam long distances

;[[Hantu]]
* Cold breath will now animate in any room during hunts if the breaker is off
* Improved cold breath visuals

;[[Obake]]
* Now has 1/15 (~6.67%) chance to appear as a different ghost model per flicker during hunts
** Will happen at least once per hunt

;[[Revenant]]
* Will now gain speed boost simply by detecting a player
* Will no longer slow down until it reaches last known player location
* Speed will now decrease by 0.75 m/s after losing player instead of instantly

;[[Wraith]]
* Will no longer step in salt piles
* Updated journal weakness

;[[Yurei]]
* Ability will no longer work if ghost is in a room without a door

=== Maps ===
* Improved campsites' performance
* Moved [[fingerprints]] on [[13 Willow Street]] doors to be more consistent
* Radios are now interactable with the breaker off
* Improved two hiding spots in [[Sunny Meadows Mental Institution]]; one by the restricted area door and one in the restricted wing
* Small tent cloth is now translucent

== General ==
=== Graphics ===
;URP
* Upgraded to Universal Render Pipeline
* New graphics options:
** Sky light - for enabling sun or moonlight
** Screen Space Ambient Occlusion
** Fog quality
** FidelityFX 1.0 - AI upscaling for better performance
*** Only compatible with AMD RX 400/Nvidia GTX 10 series GPUs and newer
* Anti-aliasing options - FXAA, SMAA or MSAA now selectable
** VR players can only select MSAA
* Improved general post-processing effects
* Transparent objects and effects will now receive shadows from lights
* DirectX 10 is no longer supported

=== Accessibility ===
* Added a text option for voice recognition
** UI will allow for asking questions on Spirit Box/Ouija Board

=== Other ===
* Swapping to a new PC or reinstalling Windows will revert the save to the player's personal cloud backup
* Resetting save will now wipe cloud backup

== Bug fixes ==
* The average sanity objective will now complete at the same time for all players
* The bone will no longer spawn inside other objects
* Tripods no longer count as electronic equipment after removing a camcorder
* Willow windows are now the correct way round
* The "Complete daily challenges" weekly challenge will now only trigger when a daily challenge completes, instead of when it progresses
* You can no longer scroll to another item while placing something
* The ghost's room will now show on the board in the truck in training
* The Deogen spirit box reply no longer requires the player to crouch
* The morgue door VR grab is now in the correct position
* Hitting the crouch button, then physically crouching in VR no longer pushes you enough into the floor that the ghost cannot kill you
* Computer monitors in [[Prison]] no longer turn on when the power is off
* The "ghost repelled" stat will now count if you are not the host
* In Sunny Meadows, the female hallway light switch will no longer toggle the utility room's light
* Journal on/off buttons are much clearer to understand
* The EULA accept button will no longer be clickable until you scroll to the bottom
* The objects moved stat will now correctly count poltergeist throws
* Your inventory will no longer break if you grab more than one item before joining a game
* Room reflections will now be accurate after turning a light and off quickly
* Russian and Ukraninian fonts will no longer have grey boxes around most characters
* VR death room spawn position is now correct
