}}The '''firelight''' is a piece of [[equipment]] in ''Phasmophobia''. It is a deployable light source that can be lit using a source of fire such as an [[igniter]] or another firelight. It can be used to continuously light up a room with a warm, orange glow, and mitigate [[sanity]] drain from darkness, until it eventually burns out.

[[Ghost]]s tend to blow out nearby firelights, making them effective at checking if a ghost is nearby. 

==Usage==
===Lighting firelights===
Players can light a firelight held in their hand by pressing {{Control|Primary Use}} if they have an igniter in their inventory. A firelight placed down can be lit while holding an igniter and pressing {{Control|Secondary Use}}.<ref group="note">There is currently a bug where lighting a placed Tier II firelight using a Tier I igniter will make it considered unlit by ghosts despite being visually lit.</ref><ref group="note">There is currently a bug where if a non-host player lights a placed Tier II firelight, it will be considered unlit by ghosts despite being visually lit.</ref> One can also hold a lit firelight, aim at an unlit firelight, and use the Secondary Use key.<ref group="note">There is currently a bug where lighting a Tier I firelight using another firelight will make it visually lit only for the player who lit it.</ref>

An unorthodox way is to have the wick of one lit and one unlit firelight to collide (this may need velocity).

===Placement===
Firelights can be {{Control|placed}} down on surfaces. Holding the placement key will show a translucent highlight of the firelight. This will place the firelight upright relative to the surface. Firelights can topple/fall over when placed on a slope or vertically.

===Function===
When a player is within 2 metres of a lit firelight, it will slow passive sanity drain that results from being in the dark, at a rate depending on the firelight's tier level. Lit firelights do not affect sanity drain from other sources such as ghost events or other players dying.

Ghosts can blow out firelights at any time, even during hunts, and this will create an [[EMF]] 2 reading (or [[EMF Level 5]]). When blown out, the firelight will be extinguished with a blowing "sigh" effect. A firelight will also extinguish by itself at the end of its duration which depends on its tier level.<ref group="note">There is currently a bug where lighting a Tier I firelight using another firelight will make it last eternally and stay lit even if the ghost blows it out.</ref> Tier I and Tier II firelights will produce smoke when blown out by the ghost or self-extinguishing. Contrary to [[Fire Source|fire sources]], however, ghosts cannot light firelights up.

Like with fire sources, when a firelight is lit up for the first time, there will be a 30-second cooldown before the ghost can attempt to blow it out. If the ghost did not blow out the firelight at the end of a cooldown, or if the firelight is not lit for the first time, a new cooldown is set with a random duration between 20 and 80 seconds.<ref group="note">There is currently a bug where the Tier II firelight's first cooldown directly has a random duration between 20 and 80 seconds instead of 30 seconds if it has been lit while held in hand.</ref> The [[Onryo]] can bypass these cooldowns due to its hunt prevention mechanics.
*Note that Tier III firelights will pause the current cooldown if they are turned off by the player or an Onryo's hunt attempt.

Ghosts have a 66.67% (2/3) chance to blow out a lit firelight provided they are within 5 meters<ref>[https://youtu.be/9dKtJk5Wx-I?t=570 A firelight blowing range test (on YouTube). 21 February 2024.]</ref><ref>[https://clips.twitch.tv/HardRamshacklePoxDxCat-VGwF3sTSZ2klCkgZ Another firelight blowing range test (on Twitch). 21 February 2024.]</ref> and the cooldown is over.
*Tier II firelights are more likely to be blown out due to them having three candles, each rolling the same 66.67% chance and if one of the three chances is successful, all three candles are blown out, roughly making it a 96.30% chance overall. Despite being multiple candles, the tier II firelight still counts as one flame for the Onryo.

{{Control|Throwing}} a lit firelight/one falling with sufficient velocity (Tiers I and II only), placing it in the inventory, leaving it out in [[Weather#Heavy Rain|heavy rain]] (Tiers I and II only), or pressing {{Control|Primary Use}} while held in hand will cause it to extinguish.

==Tips==
*Placing firelights on the sides of tables, shelves, or other elevated objects may improve their lighting by covering a wider area.
*In larger maps like [[Brownstone High School]] or [[Sunny Meadows Mental Institution]], firelights may be helpful as navigation markers for important areas such as the [[fuse box]], [[ghost room]], or [[exit door]](s), also providing some helpful sanity drain reduction as a bonus, though they are not as reliable as spare [[UV Light|UV lights]] or [[flashlight]]s, as firelights both burn out after a time (except for tier III) and can be blown out by the ghost.
*As non-electronics, firelights are less affected by hunts (other than potentially being blown out), neither do they attract the ghost. Players may use firelights as constant light sources to navigate during hunts. A potential but generally trivial downside is that it removes a source of alert as to whether the ghost is hunting (or performing ghost events).
