}}The '''0.3.1.0''' update, also known as the '''Anniversary''' update, is a minor update to the game released on 18 September 2021, in celebration of the game's one-year anniversary on Steam.<ref>https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/4972526782644554623</ref> A hotfix carrying the same version number was released later on the same day to fix some issues.<ref>https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/4972526782645080171</ref>

== Additions ==

=== Lobby ===
* New temporary decorations for anniversary update
* 10 new music tracks by Rik4k

=== Ghosts ===
* A few new sounds for ghosts

=== General ===
* New Singleplayer mode for offline play
* New fonts for several languages that had missing characters

== Changes ==
=== Gameplay ===
* Ghost orbs will now periodically change position within the ghost room
* Sillhouettes of the ghost in the [[D.O.T.S. Projector]] can no longer be seen by dead players
* Failing a contract will no longer give the player $10 and instead give insurance based on difficulty
* Failing a contract will now show what the ghost type was

=== Journal ===
{{main|Journal}}
* Journal redesigned for easier use and navigation
* Evidence can be selected or striked off individually
* Pause menu is now integrated into the journal
* Most settings can now be changed through the journal in-game

=== General ===
* VR mute button has been changed to the pause button for all controllers and the controller will vibrate when being toggled

== Bug Fixes ==

=== From versions released before 0.3.1.0 ===

* Fixed a memory leak on the main menu when using a controller
* Fixed an exploit where you could glitch your head inside something to make the ghost not see you
* Fixed several safe spots where you could stand on something and the ghost couldn't reach you
* Bleasdale: Fixed a bug where the temperature would rarely drop in the attic
* Bleasdale: Fixed a bug where the hunting equipment effects didn't always work in the attic
* Fixed a bug where the ghost whisper paranormal sound was staying active on the parabolic microphone

=== From 0.3.1.0 (as fixed in hotfix) ===

* Fixed an issue where the Mare description was wrong
* Fixed missing characters in Traditional Chinese
* Fixed text formatting for Japanese
* Fixed a bug where the push to talk button was setting the opposite option in the journal
* Fixed a bug where toggling head bob would move the camera
* Fixed a bug where pressing apply in the game settings would make your equipment be held in the wrong position
* Fixed a bug where hands were not receiving any lighting
