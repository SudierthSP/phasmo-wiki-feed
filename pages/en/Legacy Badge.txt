The '''Legacy Badge''' is a badge that was given to players that played ''Phasmophobia'' before the release of the [[0.9.0|Ascension]] update. Legacy Badges show the highest level a player achieved prior to the level system overhaul. Legacy badges are unobtainable for players that began playing after 17 August 2023.

==Features==
Upon launching the game after the [[0.9.0|Ascension]] update, eligible players are given the ability to customize their legacy badge which includes changing the color, background, and accent icons on the badge. This used to be a one-time process, and after saving the badge it would permanently show on a player's left shoulder under their [[ID Card#Prestige Badges|Prestige]]. Since version [[0.19.0]], players who have a legacy badge can customize it in the [[shop]].

==Legacy Badge Plaque==
[[File:Legacy Badge plaque 1.png|thumb|Legacy Badge Plaque.]]
As of version [[0.9.0.4]], a plaque was added to the [[Lobby]] to show a player's Legacy Badge. This will only appear for players that have a Legacy Badge.
