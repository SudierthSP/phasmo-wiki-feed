}}The '''flashlight''' is an essential piece of [[equipment]] that is used to illuminate the area, usually in front of the player.

==Usage==
{{Control|Toggling}} on an equipped flashlight will provide a small conical range of light to improve visibility of the environment. The flashlight is always held in the player's left hand and can be {{Control|dropped}} if the right hand is empty. When the right hand is not empty, the flashlight can be turned on or off by pressing the {{Control|Special}} key.

VR players can carry multiple flashlights at the same time while non-VR players can't.

Up to 4 flashlights can be brought on a single [[contract]]. One flashlight is always provided per contract as a starter equipment.

During a [[hunt]], a flashlight will rapidly flicker if a ghost is within 10 metres (15 metres if it is a [[Raiju]]) and on the same floor. Like other electronic equipment, carrying a lit flashlight within 7.5 metres of the ghost during a hunt on the same floor will attract it.

==Tips==
*Extra flashlights can be dropped within dark areas of the map to provide illumination that remains unaffected by the state of the [[fuse box]] (though is subject to electronic interference during a hunt). This may be helpful if a [[ghost]] tampers with the lights and/or [[fuse box]] frequently.
