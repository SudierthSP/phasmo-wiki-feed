The '''Parabolic Microphone''', sometimes referred to as a "paramic", is a piece of [[equipment]] in ''Phasmophobia''. It can detect sounds through walls and at a great distance, serving almost as a handheld version of the [[sound sensor]].     

==Mechanics==
The parabolic microphone allows the player to hear a [[ghost]]'s normally inaudible [[Paranormal Sound|paranormal sounds]], as well as amplifying any other sounds like object interactions. The parabolic microphone must be inside the building to detect sounds. When activated, the player will distinctively hear the sounds produced in front of the microphone with an angle of 110° horizontally, whether they are made by the ghost, another player, active equipment, or ambient noises, while all other sounds will be significantly muffled.

The Tier I and Tier II parabolic microphones will only detect sounds on the same floor as the player. 

===Ghost Sound Traits===
*[[Myling]]s make paranormal sounds more often than other ghosts.
*[[Banshee]]s have a certain chance of using a unique paranormal sound.
*[[Moroi]] will curse players who hear their paranormal sounds, resulting in an increased passive sanity drain.

===Tier I===
The Tier I Parabolic Microphone has a range of 20 meters forward. It has a red light that will flicker when it detects sound.

===Tier II===
[[File:Parabolic Display.png|thumb|222x222px|The display of the tier II Parabolic Microphone.]]
The Tier II Parabolic Microphone has a range of 30 meters forward and has an additional screen which will display three sets of numbers:
* The largest number display (000.0) measures the total sum of sound (in decibels) from all detected sources.
* The '''Min''' and '''Peak''' values display the quietest and loudest individual sound levels picked up, respectively.
** For example, if a 16 dB sound, a 17 dB sound, and a 19 dB sound are picked up, '''Min''' will display 16 dB and '''Peak''' will display 19 dB, and the sum will be 52 dB.

During a [[hunt]], the display will rapidly flash random numbers.

===Tier III===
The Tier III Parabolic Microphone is similar to the Tier II Parabolic Microphone, but it includes a sonar function, allowing its screen to display the position and the volume of each individual sound. It can also detect sounds one floor above or below the user.

==Tips ==
*The player should remain aware of other noises, such as interactions from players. Coordinate with others and communicate to know whether a sound might have originated from a player or the ghost.
*Although generally disregarded for smaller maps, it may come in handy on larger ones (such as [[Brownstone High School]] and [[Sunny Meadows Mental Institution]]) for locating the ghost by pointing players in its general direction; this becomes even more effective in a team, where two players can use a Parabolic Microphone at different locations, and pinpoint the ghost's location quite accurately.
*On large maps where a hunt might start without players even knowing, a Parabolic Microphone is a good way to provide advance warning from a far distance that a hunt has started/is in progress, and warn players to stay away from that direction.
