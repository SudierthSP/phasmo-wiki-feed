'''Radios''' are devices that can be found on several maps which the [[ghost]] can interact with.

==Mechanics==
The player and the ghost can {{Control|interact}} with radios to toggle a distorted music. They will still function even if the [[fuse box]] is turned off.

===Rewards===
{{Main|Media}}
Filming a radio being toggled by the ghost will yield a '''Distorted Music''' video in the journal, rewarding up to $7 and 7 XP, depending on the tier of the [[Video Camera]] and whether there is a Unique stamp. A '''Distorted Music''' sound may also be recorded with a [[Sound Recorder]] for the same reward.
