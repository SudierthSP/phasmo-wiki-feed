'''Balls''' are [[prop]]s found in some [[map]]s.

==Mechanics==
Balls can be {{Control|picked up}} and {{Control|thrown}} by players, and interacted with by [[ghost]]s. Some balls may have bouncy physics such as basketballs or footballs.

When a ghost [[Interaction|interacts]] with a ball, following standard interaction rules, it will throw it. If the ball is a basketball or a volleyball, it also has a chance to teleport it somewhere else in the investigation area. Both instances will create an [[EMF]] Level 2 reading on the ball, with a 33% chance of it being [[EMF Level 5]] instead if the ghost has this evidence.
