}}The '''0.29.6''' update was released on 17 June 2021 on the Beta branch and on 18 June 2021 on the main branch.<ref>https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/2980801691035865221</ref> This update adds two new ghosts, a new street map, as well as several changes and bug fixes. Two hotfixes carrying the same version were later released to fix bugs.

== Additions ==

=== Ghosts ===
{{main|Ghost}}
;[[Hantu]]
*Moves faster than most ghosts in colder rooms and slower than most ghosts in warmer rooms
*Requires [[Fingerprints]], [[Ghost Orb]], and [[Ghost Writing]] as evidence

;[[Yokai]]
*Becomes more aggressive when players are talking near it
*Has a small hearing range compared to other ghosts during hunts
*Requires [[Spirit Box]], [[Ghost Orb]], and [[Ghost Writing]] as evidence

=== Maps ===
{{main|Map}}
;[[Willow Street House]]
*Small map designed around hiding behind furniture
*10 rooms in building with first floor and basement

=== General ===
;[[Daily and Weekly Tasks|Daily Challenges]]
*New "Capture a 3 star ghost photo" and "Capture a Dirty Water photo" challenges

;Other
*New profanity filter for usernames/server names
*New Sell button to sell back items for 50% of the original price
*Name of the map is now shown on the interactive map
*New confirmation timer when resolution is changed

== Changes ==

=== Equipment ===
{{main|Equipment}}
;Smudge Sticks
*Smoke time reduced from 15 seconds to 6 seconds

;Thermometer
*Size of the text has been increased to make it easier to read in VR

=== Gameplay ===
*Falling out of bounds will now teleport the player back to their last location within the map
*Ghosts can now wander far away from its room
*Ghosts now have a higher chance to interact with its environment outside of hunts

=== Ghosts ===
;Demon
*Now has a slightly higher chance to hunt

=== Hunts ===
{{main|Hunt}}
*The ghost will now walk to the front of the closet/locker if it sees or hears the player inside rather than walking past

=== Maps ===
*High School and [[Asylum]] truck maps have been flipped to make them less confusing to navigate

;[[Brownstone High School]]
*Top and bottom floor corridors have been split into 3 sections each

;[[Prison]]
*Truck has been moved closer to the entrance

=== General ===
*Kicked players will now be automatically kicked when they re-join
*Changed how the alternative ports value is saved

== Bug Fixes ==

=== From issues introduced before 0.29.6 ===
*Improved the performance and load times on most house maps
*Fixed a bug where player voices would sometimes go high pitch
*High School: Fixed a bug where sound sensors showed the wrong corridor names
*Fixed a bug where equipment didn't teleport back to the truck if they fell out of bounds
*Fixed a bug where the ghost was changing the temperature in the wrong room
*Fixed a bug where new players would have their volume set to the old players volume
*Fixed a bug where the ghost couldn't kill you in some closets
*Fixed several safe spots
*Fixed a bug where players would still show in the audio options after they have left
*Fixed a bug where the resolution would sometimes be set very low when launching for the first time
*Fixed a bug where Sensors could be placed on walls behind other objects
*Fixed a bug where the ghost was never wandering out of it's room
*Fixed a bug where the EMF Reader would still show a value if you picked it up from the floor into your inventory
*Fixed a bug where Smudge Sticks could not be lit with a candle
*Fixed a bug where the Thermometer would sometimes show values for the wrong room

=== From 0.29.6 (as fixed in hotfixes) ===
;First hotfix
*Fixed a bug where enabling alternative ports would stop your from being able to connect to the server

;Second hotfix
*Fixed a bug where if your windows calendar was set to Thai you could not connect to the game servers
*Fixed a bug where the server names text could go outside of the box if the name was long
*Fixed a bug where daily challenges gave no reward
*Fixed several safe names that were being caught by the profanity filter
