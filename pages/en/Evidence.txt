'''Evidence''' is used to determine which [[ghost]] is haunting the [[map|location]]. Each ghost has a unique combination of three pieces of evidence that determines which ghost is present. Each player can record pieces of evidence that has been uncovered in the [[Journal]] to thin down the possible ghosts, and determine the ghost once all three piece of evidence are collected.

==Ghost evidence==
By default, each ghost can give 3 types of evidence. On Nightmare [[difficulty]], each ghost can only provide 2 types of evidence, meaning at the start of the [[contract]], the game will randomly choose 2 types, and the remaining type will be hidden (unobtainable). Similarly, Insanity difficulty causes each ghost to only give 1 type of evidence, with the other 2 being hidden. The 'Evidences given' option for a custom difficulty can also be changed for the ghost to give 0, 1, 2, or 3 types of evidence.

Some pieces of evidence are guaranteed for specific ghosts, shown as '''<u>bold and underlined</u>''' on the table, meaning that on Nightmare or Insanity difficulty (or if the 'Evidences given' option is set to 1 or 2), they will not be hidden and will ''always'' be one of the ghost's given evidences. This does not apply if the 'Evidences given' option is set to 0.

[[The Mimic]] will always produce Ghost Orbs ''in addition to'' its chosen evidences. This works differently from the above "forced" evidences, as none of the Mimic's usual evidences (Spirit Box, Ultraviolet, and Freezing Temperatures) are guaranteed to appear when 'Evidences given' is 1 or 2, while the extra Ghost Orbs are '''always''' present even when 'Evidences given' is 0.

==Trivia==
*It is not required to update all of the evidence options if the ghost has already been conclusively determined; simply selecting the correct type of ghost is sufficient.
*During the development of Phasmophobia, the presence of bugs was planned to be an evidence. This is why they appear in the early VR gameplay trailer for the game.
*There are currently five three-evidence combinations that can't appear in game:
**D.O.T.S Projector, Freezing Temperatures, Spirit Box
**D.O.T.S Projector, Freezing Temperatures, Ultraviolet
**EMF Level 5, Freezing Temperatures, Ghost Orb
**EMF Level 5, Ghost Orb, Ghost Writing
**Ghost Orb, Ghost Writing, Ultraviolet
