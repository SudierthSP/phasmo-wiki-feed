# 源: https://phasmophobia.fandom.com/api.phpGhost model is the appearance of a [[ghost]]. There are currently seven male and four female ghost models in ''Phasmophobia'', totalling 11 different ghost models. Each model can also appear as a shadowy or translucent version of itself during [[ghost event]]s.

While no singular ghost model is tied to a type of ghost, the [[Banshee]] and the [[Dayan]] are always female and can only use female ghost models, allowing players to rule them out if the ghost appears with a male model.

The model of the ghost may conflict with information gathered from the [[Spirit Box]] and [[Ouija Board]]; ghosts labeled as "old" may have one of the models that one would equate to a child, for example.

==Postures==
The child and "Patient 07" models have multiple postures that they can switch into during a [[contract]]. In addition to the standing posture, both of these models can be crawling, and bent over backwards for the "Patient 07" model.

==Themed event models==
{{See also|Special events}}
For Christmas 2021, one of the ghost models was given a temporary Santa Claus garb, accompanied by ringing bells as footstep sounds. During Christmas 2022, the ghost would also transform into this model after eating the cookies.

For Halloween 2022, various ghost models wore a jack-o-lantern on their head.

On April Fools' 2023, all ghosts had a bigger head.

For Christmas 2023, the ghost would transform into a snowman after all dancing snowmen of the map were destroyed. Its footsteps were accompanied by ringing bells.

For Easter 2024, the ghost on [[Camp Woodwind]] would become a Forest Spirit after all five of its minions are photographed. The Forest Spirit came back for Easter 2025 and would appear on maps featuring the ''Cursed Hollow'' event.

For the ''Winter's Jest'' event, ghosts on specific maps used a special Krampus model. This model is considered male.

During the Phasmophobia by Alan Wake event, the ghost had a cultist model, wearing a raincoat and a deer mask.
