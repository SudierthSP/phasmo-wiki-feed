During the investigation, players can record '''media''' for additional money and experience rewards. Recorded media can be seen in the Media tab in the [[journal]]. 

==Mechanics==
During a [[contract]], when a subject of a specific category is recorded for the first time using the [[Photo Camera]], the [[Video Camera]], or the [[Sound Recorder]], it is marked with a '''Unique''' stamp. Recording another subject of the same category will register it as a '''Duplicate''' instead, rewarding less [[money]] and [[experience]].

A bonus is awarded per piece of media when recorded with a higher tier of the corresponding equipment:
*Tier II: $1 / 1 XP
*Tier III: $2 / 2 XP

Recorded sounds can be listened back in the Media tab in the journal.

In order to complete a Perfect Investigation, alongside usual requirements, the journal must be filled with only Unique media, which include 5 Unique photos, 5 Unique videos, and 3 Unique sounds.
