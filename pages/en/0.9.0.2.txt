==Changes==

* Save files will now be backed up more often

==Fixes==

* If you have a corrupt save file you will no longer get stuck on initializing and will now revert to your cloud backup
* Non hosts will no longer crash when a player leaves the game
* When you play this update for the first time you should no longer have a low legacy level on your legacy badge

To clear up some confusion with legacy badge, your badge can currently only be seen in multiplayer by another player on your character. This will change in a future update when we add new character models.
