}}'''0.10.1.2''' is a hotfix released on 15 August 2024.<ref>[https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/6692998230483065042 ''Photo Deletion and Vivox | Hotfix v0.10.1.2''. 15 August 2024 (On Steam).]</ref> It fixes several important bugs with the new voice system.

==Fixes==
*The ghost can now hear Steam Deck players
*Voice chat will now work on Steam Deck
*The gamepad cursor is no longer offset in the journal
*If you mute a player, they will now be muted in global chat
*You can now play if you fail to connect to the voice server
*The ghost can now hear you if you change your microphone in singleplayer
*Photos can now be removed if a player leaves

==Known Issues==
*Vosk voice recognition does not work on Steam Deck
