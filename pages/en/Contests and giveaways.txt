===VIP Giveaway (2022)===
This series of contests and giveaways were hosted individually by Kinetic Games' official content creators. The winners or selected recipients of said contests/giveaways would receive an in-game badge.

Starting today watch your favourite official Phasmophobia content creator to have a chance at winning an in-game VIP badge! 

See below for more details.

[[File:VIP Contest Announcement.jpg|440px]]

! colspan="4" |Contest / Giveaway Details

| colspan="4" | 
Each Official Content Creator was given 3 VIP cards to distribute by whatever means they chose. Many creators chose to make their distribution more of a contest (video essays, bingo tournaments, last man standing, etc.) while others truly did a giveaway (selecting someone random from their streams, holding raffles, giving to friends, etc.)

The exact dates and times of each content creator's contest/giveaway was up to the creator. All creators were to give their chosen recipients' Steam IDs to Kinetic Games so they could associate the appropriate role with those accounts.

There was no official announcement from Kinetic Games of the chosen recipients, so a complete list of winners is not publicly known. The chosen recipients received their VIP ID cards in-game on 18 January 2023.

===Blood Moon Art Contest (2024)===
This contest was hosted by Kinetic Games on the official Phasmophobia Discord Server. The winner and runners up would receive an in-game badge, a Steam gift card, the Community Artist Discord Role, and 1 Phasmophobia game key.

A Phasmophobia Halloween art contest!

To celebrate the release of the new Crimson Eye update, the Blood Moon Halloween event and console updates we are looking for the artistically-inclined ghost-hunting fans of Phasmophobia to put their pens to their spirit-books and create some fan art!

Should your spooky images impress the ghosts trapped in this haunted house and capture the spirit of the game, you could win fun and exciting prizes!

So whether you're an experienced and hardened ghost-hunter, or a bright-eyed and optimistic new hunter stepping foot into the lighthouse for the first time, impress us with your ghostly art as you created spooky images Under the Blood Moon!

<div class="mw-collapsible mw-collapsed">
;RULES
For the best chance to win, try to incorporate the essence of the Blood Moon!
*Must involve in part or in whole Phasmophobia and Halloween
*Strictly no AI art is allowed in this contest.
*Can be classically drawn/painted, digital art, 3d renderings, or illustrations. If you are using a screenshot from the game it must only be used as a base layer that you’ve built up into a creative new image
*No nudity
*While some blood/gore is allowed due to the nature of the game, please keep it tasteful!
*Any additional classic Phasmophobia elements are encouraged!
*No previous art experience required! We're looking for any art that captures the spirit of Halloween and Phasmophobia!
*Please keep the max resolution for your entry at or below 1440p

;PRIZES
'''1st place'''
*£100 Steam gift card
*In-game VIP ID card
*Community Artist Discord Role
*1 Phasmophobia game key (platform of your choice!)

'''3 runners ups'''
*£50 Steam gift card
*In-game VIP ID card
*Community Artist Discord Role
*1 Phasmophobia game key (platform of your choice!)

9 additional creators will get the Community Artist Discord Role.
</div>
