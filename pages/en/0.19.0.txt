}}'''0.19.0''', also known as the '''Quality of Life Update Part 2''', is a major update released on 25 August 2026.<ref>[https://www.kineticgames.co.uk/news/phasmophobia-v01900-quality-of-life-part-2 ''Phasmophobia v0.19.0.0 - Quality of Life Part 2''. 25 August 2026.]</ref><ref group="note">Official Kinetic Games Partners were given early access keys on 24 August 2026.</ref> This update brings quality of life improvements.

==Additions==
*Added new Restricted variants of the below maps.
**[[Prison]]
**[[Brownstone High School]]
**[[Point Hope]]
*Video Cameras, Photo Cameras and Sound Recorders will now indicate Duplicate evidence.
**Tier 1 Photo Cameras will display a yellow LED light.
**Tier 2 & 3 Photo Cameras and all Tiers of Video Cameras will display faded text rather than the usual bold white text.
**Tier 1 Sound Recorder will lower the brightness of the Volume indicators.
**Tier 2 & 3 Sound Recorders will display faded sound waves.
*The Journal will now display other player selections of Evidence and Ghost Type.
*The Keys section in the Journal has been replaced with Items We’ve Found, this new section will show keys, the bone and seasonal event collectables.
*Added the ability to select the Ghost Type in a Custom Difficulty which will set the multiplier to 0.
*Added a new Legacy Badge section to the Customisation shop to allow you to edit your badge at any time.
**''Players without a Legacy Badge will not see this option.''
*Added a ‘Hold Use’ sticky note on the Training whiteboards.
*Dead players and dead player bodies will now look dead.
*Added unique video evidence text for shower and fuse box interactions.
*Added an icon to show whether a ghost name is male or female.
*The Evidence page will now highlight the required Ghost evidence when the player selects a Ghost type.

==Changes==
*Photo and Video Camera screens will now disable when clipping through a wall
*When speaking, the speaker icon on ID cards will now function for all players, including local player
*Improvements have been made to the VR character rig, allowing for more accurate and responsive movement.
*Players can now interact with a Spirit Box when not grabbed using Text Voice Recognition Mode
*Custom Difficulties with a 0 multiplier will no longer contribute towards any progress, such as achievements and leaflets.
*Pressing use while holding a lit T1 Igniter will now use another, without having to wait for the animation to finish.
*When a player dies, their voice chat will now be cut out as soon as they die in the animation, rather than at the end.
*The Truck has been moved closer to the main entrance on Brownstone High School and Prison
