[[File:Difficulty Selection.png|thumb|300x300px|The contract difficulty selection screen]]'''Difficulties''' in ''Phasmophobia'' adjust the player experience according to their skill. There are five main '''difficulties''' in ''Phasmophobia'': Amateur, Intermediate, Professional, Nightmare, and Insanity. Custom difficulties can also be created. Difficulties impact the player, the ghost, and the contract, giving out varying rewards.

==Difficulties==
{{#tag:tabber|
Amateur=
<blockquote>''"Recommended for new Ghost Hunters!"''</blockquote>This is the simplest difficulty aimed at players who are new to the game and are slowly learning the mechanics.
*Normal rewards
*5-minute setup time
*Frequent ghost activity
*Shortest hunting phase
*No doors will start open
*[[Sanity Medication]] restores 40% of [[sanity]]
*50% [[Death#Equipment & Insurance|value of lost items returned]] to dead players

{{!}}-{{!}}
Intermediate=
[[File:Intermediate Difficulty.jpg|thumb|100px]]
<blockquote>''"The standard Ghost Huntin' experience"''</blockquote>This difficulty is for the average player who has a good knowledge of the game's mechanics.
*Unlocked at level 10
*2x rewards
*2-minute setup time
*1.5x sanity consumption
*Common ghost activity
*Medium hunting phase
*Sanity Medication restores 35% of sanity
*Few doors will start open
*25% value of lost items returned to dead players
*The [[fuse box]] will be off at the start of the game
*Some hiding locations will be blocked

{{!}}-{{!}}
Professional=
[[File:Professional Difficulty.jpg|thumb|100px]]
<blockquote>''"For experienced Ghost Hunters"''</blockquote>This difficulty is for experienced players who have a relatively strong foundation in the core mechanics, ghosts, and strategies needed to complete the investigation.
*Unlocked at level 20
*3x rewards
*No setup time
*2x sanity consumption
*Uncommon ghost activity
*Long hunting phase
*Sanity Medication restores 30% of sanity
*0% value of lost items returned to dead players
*The Fuse Box will be off at the start of the game
*The [[Objective Board|objective board]] will not specify if the ghost responds to everyone or people who are alone
*The [[ghost room]] will change rarely
*Some doors will start open
*Most hiding locations will be blocked

{{!}}-{{!}}
Nightmare=
[[File:Nightmare Difficulty.jpg|thumb|100px]]
<blockquote>''"A challenge for the best of the best"''</blockquote>This difficulty is a challenge aimed solely at players who know the game's mechanics inside-out, have strong practical experience in ghost hunting, and have a solid strategy or action plan, especially in teams.
*Unlocked at level 30
*4x rewards
*No setup time
*2x sanity consumption
*Uncommon ghost activity
*Longest hunting phase
*Sanity Medication restores 25% of sanity
*0% value of lost items returned to dead players
*The fuse box will be off at the start of the game
*The Objective Board will not specify if the ghost responds to everyone or people who are alone
*The ghost room will change occasionally
*The [[Sanity Monitor]] and [[Site Activity Monitor]] will be damaged and display random values
*Almost all hiding locations will be blocked
*Many doors will start open
*The fuse box is not shown on the truck map
*A successful kill will extend the [[hunt]]ing phase, allowing multiple kills in a single hunt
*The ghost will only provide 2 pieces of evidence instead of 3

{{!}}-{{!}}
Insanity=
[[File:Insanity Difficulty.jpg|thumb|100px]]
<blockquote>''"Feeling brave? These ghosts don't mess around..."''</blockquote>This difficulty is an extremely hard challenge solely aimed at players that have mastered the mechanics of the game from top to bottom. Players must have knowledge of all ghost strengths, weaknesses, abilities and behaviors and have knowledge on many hunting and escape tactics on the maps.
*Unlocked at level 40
*6x rewards
*No setup time
*No Cursed Possession
*Sanity starts at 75%
*2x sanity consumption
*Uncommon ghost activity
*Longest hunting phase
*Sanity Medication restores 20% of sanity
*0% value of lost items returned to dead players
*The fuse box will be off at the start of the game
*The fuse box is not shown on the truck map
*The Objective Board will not specify if the ghost responds to everyone or people who are alone
*The ghost room will change frequently
*The [[Sanity Monitor]] and [[Site Activity Monitor]] will be damaged and display random values
*Almost all hiding locations will be blocked
*Many doors will start open
*A successful kill will extend the hunting phase
*The ghost will only provide 1 piece of evidence
*Fingerprints last for 60 seconds

{{!}}-{{!}}
Challenge Mode=
{{Main|Challenge Mode}}
A unique difficulty with a customised equipment loadout and possibly other difficulty changes is available every week. There are currently 26 possible challenge mode difficulties. Free equipment is provided specifically for Challenge Mode. Completing a Challenge Mode difficulty on a specified map 3 times before the difficulty changes rewards the player with $5000 and 5000 XP.

{{!}}-{{!}}
Custom=
[[File:Custom Difficulty.jpg|thumb|100px]]<br>
Unlocked at level 50, this difficulty lets the player choose the settings for the contract, with up to 3 custom difficulties that can be saved. Options pertaining to the player, ghost, and contract can be changed.
;Player
*Starting sanity
*Sanity Medication restoration amount
*Sanity drain speed
*Sprinting use allowance
*Player speed
*Flashlights being broken
*Losing items on death toggle (no rewards)

;Ghosts
*Ghost type
*Ghost speed
*Roaming frequency
*Chance of changing favourite room
*Activity level
*Event frequency
*Disable hunts (no rewards)
*Grace period duration
*Hunt duration
*Kills extending hunts
*Number of evidences given
*Fingerprint chance
*Fingerprint duration

;Contract
*Setup time
*Weather
*Number of doors starting open
*Amount of hiding places
*Sanity monitor state
*Activity monitor state
*Fuse box starting state
*Fuse box map visibility
*Cursed Possessions quantity
*Cursed Possession choice(s)

===Presets===
When editing a custom difficulty, some presets can be chosen, five of which are the default difficulties (Amateur to Insanity), three are the currently saved custom difficulties, and three are for the [[Apocalypse Challenge]], corresponding to the Bronze (6x), Silver (10x) and Gold (15x) trophies.

The Apocalypse presets are only suggestions and do not have to be followed to obtain the trophy as long as the multiplier is equal to or higher than the minimum required.
