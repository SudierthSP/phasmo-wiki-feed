The '''photo camera''' is a piece of [[equipment]] in ''Phasmophobia''. It is a digital camera that can be used to take pictures of objects in-game.  While it is not used for gathering [[evidence]], its main use is to gather [[Media#Photo evidence|photo evidence]], as well as being necessary for certain [[objectives]] and [[Daily and Weekly Tasks|tasks]].

==Usage==
[[File:Photocamera_active.png|right|300px|thumb|A held active tier II photo camera.]]
The photo camera automatically turns on while held outside the truck. When a valid subject is in the camera's frame within 3.5 metres, or within 8 metres for the [[ghost]], the Tier I's red light indicator will turn green while a highlight frame will show on the Tiers II and III's screen, indicating that said subject can be taken in photo. If a subject is considered as a Duplicate, the Tier I's red light indicator will turn yellow and the Tier II and III's highlight frame's text will be faded.

Upon taking a picture, the flash will go off, and a photo preview will show up on the camera for Tier II and III cameras. Any pictures taken of a reflective surface will show the lens flash in the picture, such as a television, or the axe that one of the possible [[Ghost Model|ghost models]] wields. The cooldown between photos depends on the Tier of the photo camera (3 seconds for Tier I, 2 seconds for Tier II, and 1 second for Tier III).

Each photo camera can take an unlimited number of photos, but only the 5 pictures recorded in the [[journal]] will count towards earning rewards and completing objectives.

When a ghost manifests during a [[ghost event]] or a [[hunt]], the photo camera's display will be distorted, as well as any pictures taken. Pictures taken in this state will still be labeled in the [[journal]] if a valid subject is detected.

Holding a Tier II or III photo camera within 7.5 metres of a ghost (2.5 metres for the [[Yokai]]) during a hunt on the same floor will attract the ghost. The Tier I photo camera will only attract the ghost when a picture is taken.

===Rewards===
{{Main|Media}}
The first 5 labeled pictures recorded in the journal will reward the player with [[money]] and [[experience]]. Like other media evidence, the value of photos is determined by its ''Unique'' or ''Duplicate'' tag. While the first photo of a subject will grant full reward, additional photos of the same subject taken during the same contract will provide only 20% of the normal reward.

If there are no available slots for photos inside the journal (which can hold 5 photos for the whole team), any further shots will not net any gains.

If a shot is missed or judged unsatisfactory, players have the option to delete up to three pieces of media from the journal. In multiplayer, a majority vote from the players is required to delete a photo, signaled by a red icon on the ''Media'' tab of the Journal.

==Trivia==
*Captured photos are intentionally not synced over with other players, and are instead taken and rendered individually on each client to improve performance. This can cause confusion, as this often leads to the  photo angle being different and causing objects to be in frame for one person but not so for another.
*The real-life camera models for each tier are:
**'''Tier I''': Polaroid OneStep 600 Flash (Polaroid "rebranded" as Polteroid)
**'''Tier II''': Sony W800 compact camera (Sony "rebranded" as Bony)
**'''Tier III''': Sony a7RV mirrorless camera body + Sony SEL2470GM2 lens (Sony "rebranded" as Bony)
