The '''thermometer''' is a piece of starter [[equipment]] used to read the [[temperature]]s in the nearby environment, and is the primary tool for detecting [[Freezing Temperatures]].

==Usage==
===Tier I===
The Tier I Thermometer passively reads out the temperature of the last room in which the player held it, with the red liquid (alcohol) within slowly rising or falling depending on if the room is colder or warmer than where it was before. While very slow to update, with a rate of 0.33°C per second, it benefits over its upgraded versions by being both completely passive, and is not an electronic item that could attract the [[ghost]] while held during a [[hunt]].

The thermometer reads out both Celsius (°C) and Fahrenheit (°F), with Celsius values on the left side and Fahrenheit on the right. The reading will fluctuate from the actual value by a maximum of +3 degrees, in Celsius.

===Tier II===
The thermometer can be {{Control|toggled}} to turn on the display. Holding down the {{Control|use}} button will make the thermometer read the player's current room's temperature in degrees Celsius (°C) every 2.5 seconds. This can be changed to Fahrenheit (°F) in the settings. The reading will fluctuate from the actual value by a maximum of +3 degrees, in Celsius.

===Tier III===
Similar to the Tier II thermometer, it can be {{Control|toggled}} to turn on the display, and can be used to give a reading by holding down the use button. Compared to the Tier II thermometer, it is much faster to give a read-out, only taking 1.5 seconds between each reading. 

By default, the reading will be in degrees Celsius (°C); this can be changed to Fahrenheit (°F) in the settings. The reading will fluctuate from the actual value by a maximum of +2 degrees, in Celsius.

==Tips==
*Temperatures readings below the weather's ambient temperature (e.g. below 5°C in snowy weather) is generally an indication that the ghost is in (or was recently in) the room.
*The tier II and III thermometers will continuously give out new readings when players hold {{Control|Use}} after the initial reading while the tier I thermometer will refresh constantly, although at a much slower speed.
*When using thermometers thrown on the floor, turning it off and on again or moving between rooms before attempting to read the temperature may help to reduce the risk of errors.

==Trivia==
*The tier III thermometer is actually a Therma-Hygrometer which can also measure the air humidity; the design of the tier III thermometer is based off the THS-224-612 Therma-Hygrometer.
*The tier III thermometer displays "EMS 0.95" on its screen, but EMS (Emissivity) is a measure for thermal radiation, and is used for infrared thermometers like the tier II thermometer. Since the tier III thermometer uses airflow to measure, the device is inaccurate.
