}}The '''0.5.0''' update, also known as the '''Cursed Possessions''' update, is a content update to the game that was released on 10 December 2021.<ref>https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630?emclan=103582791466524086&emgid=3099036308542409852</ref> This update adds several equipment related to interacting with the ghost, one new ghost, as well as a rework of the existing ghosts' behaviours.

== Additions ==

=== Equipment ===
{{Main|Equipment}}
==== Cursed possessions ====
{{Main|Cursed possession}}
*1 of 6 items will spawn per [[contract]], with several spawnpoints
*Will trade sanity or other negative effects for information or uses

;[[Music Box]]
*When activated, can sometimes cause the ghost to sing along to the melody, which can make identifying the ghost room easier.

;[[Summoning Circle]]
*Used to summon the ghost, leading up to a hunt

;[[Tarot Cards]]
*Contains ten randomly-selected cards, with positive or negative effects

;[[Haunted Mirror]]
*Can be used to locate the current ghost room, at the cost of severe sanity drain the longer it is used

=== Gameplay ===
;[[Journal]]
*New Home and Item tabs

=== Ghosts ===
{{main|Ghost}}
;[[The Mimic]]
*May mimic the actions of other ghosts
*Ghost Orbs may appear as a fourth evidence
*Requires Spirit Box, Fingerprints, and Freezing Temperatures as evidence

== Changes ==

=== Equipment ===
;[[Voodoo Doll]]
*Can now used to force the ghost to perform interactions
*Updated visuals

;[[Ouija Board]]
*Updated visuals
*Now classed as a cursed possession
*Now requires user to say goodbye to the board
*New questions related to bone location, method of death,
*No longer fails questions
*No longer plays an additional sound when spelling double letters
*Spawn chance is now treated as a cursed posession

;[[Parabolic Microphone]]
*Paranormal sounds can now be heard more often

;[[Photo Camera]] and [[Video Camera]]
*Interference effect during manifestations has been adjusted

===Evidence and ghost traces===
;[[Ghost Writing]]
*Ghosts will now also interact with it when it throws the book outside of hunts

;[[Fingerprints]]
*Photos of "refreshed" fingerprints will now count as a [[Media|photo reward]]

===Gameplay===
*Props/objects within the house now have different impact sounds to match its material
*New sounds for player footsteps on different surfaces
*Large rugs and floor mats now have collision
*Interaction photos of objects thrown must now be of the objective itself instead of where the object was beforehand
*Light flickers will now be based on distance of the ghost to the light instead of the switch

===Ghosts===
;General
*Will now use abilities more often and more consistently
*Will now consider distance of path to take when setting waypoints
*Heartbeats now scale depending on the distance of the player to the ghost

;[[Banshee]]
*No longer has rare ability to hunt at any sanity
*Will choose singing ghost events more often
*Can emit unique paranormal sound audible to the parabolic microphone

;[[Demon]]
*Now has a rare ability to hunt at any sanity
*Now has shorter hunt cooldown of 20 seconds

;[[Hantu]]
*Will now emit freezing breath when manifested

;[[Jinn]]
*Distance for speed drop-off decreased

;[[Mare]]
*May now turn off lights more often, including instantly
*Will choose dark rooms to wander into more often
*Will choose light bulb explosion ghost events more often

;[[Oni]]
*Will now always manifest during ghost events
*More likely to show up in full form during ghost events

;[[Shade]]
*Hunt sanity threshold reduced to 35%
*Will now have less interactions regardless of player numbers

;[[Yokai]]
*Now reacts more to player voices

;[[Yurei]]
*New ability to close doors while draining sanity from players in the same room

===Hunts===
*Players can no longer back out through the [[Exit Door|exit doors]] when the hunt starts
*Ghosts can now throw objects during hunts
*Ghosts will now only use physical form during hunts
*Ghosts can now hear the player's voice from further away
*Hunting sounds will now fade out instead of stopping abruptly

===Maps===
;[[Maple Lodge Campsite]]
*New temporary visuals for winter season
*Assets have been optimised

===General===
*Several scripts and [[weather]] has been optimised for performance
