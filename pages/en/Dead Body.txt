A '''dead body''' is the corpse of a player that was [[death|killed]] by the [[ghost]] during a [[hunt]], drew ''The Hanged Man'' [[Tarot Cards|tarot card]] or died from the negative effect of the revival wish from the [[Monkey Paw|monkey paw]]. It will remain at that player's place of death until the end of the game or their resurrection.

==Mechanics==
During the process of death, the player who died will levitate with their hands reaching their neck, before having it snapped and dropping dead.

The player can be revived by drawing [[Tarot Cards|tarot cards]], via the ''The High Priestess'' card, or using the [[Monkey Paw]]'s revival wish (which also has a 50% chance to simultaneously kill the wisher).

===Rewards===
{{Main|Media}}
Taking a photo of a dead body or filming the [[death]] of a player will respectively yield a '''Dead Body''' photo rewarding up to $7 and 7 XP, and a '''Dead Body''' video rewarding up to $12 and 12 XP, depending on the equipment tier and whether there is a Unique stamp.

==Trivia==
*Taking a photo of the dead body does not affect the dead player's insurance.
*There used to be a bug where taking a photo of the bin outside [[42 Edgefield Road]] would count as a dead body photo.
