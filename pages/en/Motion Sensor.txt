}}The '''motion sensor''' is a piece of [[equipment]] that can help in tracking the movement of the [[ghost]].

==Usage==
A motion sensor can used by {{Control|placing}} it. After being placed, it can be turned off by {{Control|interacting}} with it.

===Tier I===
The Tier I Motion Sensor can either be placed on the wall or on the floor. When placed, the sensor will emit a red laser beam, indicating its effective detection distance. If it is placed on a wall, it will also display on the [[Site Map]] in the [[truck]] as a red bar.

When a [[player]] (even if they are dead and spectating), the ghost, the [[Ghost Event#Mist Form|mist form]], or an object passes through its laser beam, the motion sensor will emit a bright light for 2 seconds. The bar on the site map will also turn green for 6 seconds, and an audible beep will play from the monitor. Motion sensors that are not displayed will still play the beep.

===Tier II===
The Tier II Motion Sensor is similar to the Tier I Motion Sensor and adds a conical mode which can be switched to by {{Control|interacting}} with it. The conical mode allows the sensor to have a second laser beam that can help players track the ghost's movement more accurately, as its lights will be emitted from left to right, or from right to left, depending on the direction of the movement. Additionally, the Tier II Motion Sensor will directly emit an audible beep when it detects movement.

===Tier III===
The Tier III Motion Sensor can only be placed on the floor and has a 1.5-metre spherical range that is displayed as a red circle on the site map in the truck. When the ghost enters this range, the sensor will quickly rotate to the location it detected the ghost, and will emit a small light and an audible beep. On the site map, the red circle will turn green for 6 seconds. When the ghost exits the range, the sensor will only rotate to the location it detected the ghost and emit a light, without beeping.

Only the ghost can trigger this sensor.

===Rewards===
{{Main|Media}}
Filming a motion sensor being triggered by the ghost will yield a '''Motion Sensed''' video, rewarding up to $7 and 7 XP, depending on the tier of the [[Video Camera]] and whether there is a Unique stamp.

==Tips==
*Placing motion sensors at corners or room boundaries can help to track where the ghost is.
