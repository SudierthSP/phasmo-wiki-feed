}}'''0.3.0.3''' is a minor update to the game released on 3 September 2021,<ref>https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/2940276088438770053</ref> with a same-version hotfix released the following day.<ref>https://discord.com/channels/435431947963990026/661365069006897154/883516164532211712</ref> The update mainly fixes bugs.

== Changes ==
=== Equipment ===
;[[D.O.T.S. Projector]]
*Sound will now flicker randomly instead of when the ghost interacts with it
*An interaction by the ghost will no longer produce EMF

;[[Glowstick]]
*Brightness after its one minute use time has been reduced

=== Gameplay ===
*VR: Movement speed will no longer change based on the rotation of the player controller and will now only be based on the joystick/trackpad
*VR: Journal can now be grabbed with both hands again as well and UI can be interacted with using the player's left hand

=== Maps ===
;General
*Removed most house candles due to performance issues

== Bug fixes ==
=== From versions released before 0.3.0.3 ===
*Fixed an issue where the Goryo's strength wasn't functioning correctly
*Fixed a bug where the player could see the night vision light when wearing a head camera
*Fixed a bug where the cameras still had the jitter post processing effect
*Fixed an issue where the Spirit Box emission/ bloom was too bright
*Fixed a bug where clicking on locked store equipment would stop the player from buying other equipment if the player was too low a level
*Fixed a bug where the player could make the photo camera flash without taking a photo
*Fixed a bug where the player would get very low fps on the main menu after a game if using a gamepad
*Fixed a bug where standing near a DOTS Projectors would stop the player's sanity drain
*Fixed a spot in the Willow bathroom where the player could get stuck
*Fixed some missing translations on the main menu
*Fixed a few areas on Asylum where the ghost could get stuck behind the wall
*Fixed a bug where the player could hold the Tripod with a camera on during a hunt and not get chased
*Fixed and improved the gamepad navigation on the Journal
*Fixed a bug where the player would get teleported back to the van if the player went out of bounds
*Fixed a bug where graphics options were not being applied to the dead processing effects
*Fixed a bug where sensors positions would desync in multiplayer after being grabbed from a wall
*Fixed a bug where walkie talkie player volumes were too loud
*Fixed a bug where the player's voice chat microphone wouldn't be set until the player restarted the game
*Fixed a bug where other players equipment in their inventory wouldn't drop when they died
*VR: Fixed a bug where teleport grab wouldn't always grab objects
*VR: Fixed an issue where it was hard to grab objects with teleport grab
*VR: Fixed a bug where if the player teleport-grabbed a placed DOTS Projector it wouldn't turn off
*VR: Fixed grab rotations for the flashlights, cameras and smudge sticks
*VR: Fixed a bug where where the player could take a photo in the truck when the camera was off
*VR: Fixed an issue where the Thermometer text was hard to read
*VR: Fixed a bug where the player couldn't hear parabolic sounds if they held the parabolic microphone in their left hand

=== From 0.3.0.3 (as fixed in hotfix) ===
*Fixed an issue with VR players not being able to close the truck door in multiplayer unless they were the host
