}}'''0.3.0.2''' is a minor update to the game released on 29 August 2021.<ref>https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/2939149643333137559</ref> The update mainly fixes bugs.

== Changes ==
=== Equipment ===
;[[D.O.T.S. Projector]]
*The ghost will now move through the DOTS Projector at different speeds each time

== Bug fixes ==
*Fixed a bug where video cameras would turn off in the player's hand if the CCTV monitor changed camera
*Fixed a bug where the CCTV monitor would freeze if the player walked out of the truck with a tripod
*Fixed a bug where the DOTS Projector interaction would only work if the host placed it
*Fixed an issue where the DOTS Projector interaction was happening twice as often if the player placed two down
*Fixed a spot where the player could get stuck in the Ridgeview bedroom
*Fixed several performance issues with the video cameras
*VR: Fixed grab rotations for the tripod and flashlights
*VR: Fixed a bug where video cameras weren't opening after being grabbed
*VR: Fixed a bug where video cameras weren't staying open when placed on a tripod
*Fixed a bug where the player couldn't see VR players' head mounted cameras
