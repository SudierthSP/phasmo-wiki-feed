This page lists all ''Phasmophobia'' '''achievements''' or '''trophies''' that can be obtained.

There are currently 48 normal achievements, and 6 hidden achievements, for a total of 54 achievements, 24 of which are ghost specific achievements, which are obtained by successfully guessing the ghost and surviving. An additional trophy is rewarded upon obtaining all 54 trophies on PlayStation.

Unless a difficulty is specifically required for one, all achievements can be obtained on any difficulty above 0x.

Once an achievement is obtained, a corresponding trophy also appears in the [[lobby]]'s display cases.
