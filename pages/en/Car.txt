A '''car''' is one of the map features found in some [[map]]s.

==Mechanics==
All suburban houses have a garage containing at least one car or vehicle. Some houses have more than one vehicle in the garage; only one of these can be interacted with by players and [[ghost]]s.

The ghost can randomly set off the vehicle alarm, following standard [[interaction]] rules. This will create an [[EMF]] Level 2 reading, with a 33% chance of it being [[EMF Level 5]] instead if the ghost has this evidence. The alarm cannot be activated during [[hunt]]s.

The alarm persists until the players deactivate it by {{Control|interacting}} with the vehicle while having the car [[key]], which is always located in the first room when entering the house. Interacting with the vehicle while the alarm is inactive causes it to beep.
