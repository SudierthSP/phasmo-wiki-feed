It was essentially the direct upgrade to the [[flashlight]], provinding a much larger illumination radius, but retained the same capabilities as its weaker counterpart. Since [[0.9.0]], it is no longer a standalone item and now makes for the Tier II upgrade for the flashlight.

== Usage ==
{{See also|Flashlight}}
Strong flashlights are functionally the same as regular flashlights, with the exception of an increased area of effect, and a white shade of light instead of yellow. They could be purchased from the shop for $50, starting at Level 3.
