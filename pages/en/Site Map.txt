The '''Site Map '''is a screen located within the starting [[Truck]], being the top left screen. Beyond giving the name and layout of the current [[map]], it also highlights many map details, including:

* The present location of players, coded to their color on the [[Sanity Monitor]], additionally giving a small light glow to indicate which direction they're facing
* The positioning of [[Video Feed|CCTV cameras]]
* The location of the fuse box, depending on [[Difficulty]]

Depending on the map, the Site Map may either be a birds-eye view of the whole area, including outside sections, with visible props, textures and lighting (all maps added or reworked since [[0.4.0]]); or a simplistic, greyed-out outline of only the investigation area's general layout and geometry (all older maps). In the case of maps where only the geometry is given, a few further points of interest are highlighted:

* Windows, which are marked with a red line
* [[Exit Door|Exit doors]], which are marked with a green line
* Staircases to different floors, indicated with a simplistic graphic and an arrow pointing towards the direction that the stairs are accessed to reach the next floor.

On both styles of the Site Map, a couple pieces of [[Equipment]] are visible on it when placed:

* [[Motion Sensor|Motion Sensors]], which show a red bar extending a short distance outward. The bar will flash green and make an audible beep when the motion sensor is activated.
* [[Sound Sensor|Sound Sensors]], which produce a translucent orange sphere showing its effective area.

Two arrows can be found on the left side of the screen, which can be used by left-clicking on them to switch to either the next floor up or down. These arrows do not "wrap around" when reaching the uppermost or lowermost floor; the opposing arrow must be clicked to switch floors.
