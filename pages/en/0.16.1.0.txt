}}'''0.16.1.0''' is a minor update released on 31 March 2026.<ref>[https://www.kineticgames.co.uk/news/phasmophobia-cursed-hollow-v01610 ''Phasmophobia - Cursed Hollow v0.16.1.0''. 31 March 2026.]</ref><ref group="note">Official Kinetic Games Partners were given early access keys on 30 March 2026.</ref> This update reintroduces the [[Special events#Easter 2026|Cursed Hollow event]].

==Additions==
*'''Cursed Hollow:''' Complete contracts, optional objectives and more to earn points towards both Community & Personal goals. Each day, a different selection of locations will be specified on the contract board to be a part of the event.
*'''Double XP & Rewards:''' For the first week of the event (31st March - 7th April), players will receive double XP and rewards from their contracts.
*'''Cursed Hollow Rewards:''' On completing the goals, players will be rewarded with event-specific rewards such as the Pathfinder ID Card & Badge, in both static and animated versions.

==Changes==
*When revealing a ‘lucky’ Forest Minion, the ghost speed will decrease by 10%. This is stackable.
*When revealing an ‘unlucky’ Forest Minion, the ghost speed will increase by 10%. This is stackable.
*The ghost can now interact with the 6 Tanglewood Drive train set horn.
*Reduced the volume & frequency of the dog audio on 6 Tanglewood Drive.
*Reduced the volume of the rain hitting the windows in 6 Tanglewood Drive.
*Updated ghost interact sounds for doors, fridges, toilet, cupboards in 6 Tanglewood Drive.
*Added new house ambient audio in 6 Tanglewood Drive.

==Fixes==
*The Cursed Hollow Altar in the warehouse will now transform correctly upon certain goals being achieved by the community and player.
*Fixed an issue on 6 Tanglewood Drive that caused rain during Blood Moon weather to not have any audio.
*Fixed an issue that caused fingerprints on 6 Tanglewood Drive to display at the wrong size.
*Various other bug fixes and improvements.

==Known Issues==
*Forest Minion jumping sound is too loud.
