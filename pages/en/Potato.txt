}}[[File:Hoh.ogg|thumb|The impact sound the potato makes.]]The '''potato''' is a [[prop]] and an easter egg reference to the popular ''Phasmophobia'' content creator [https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCwC6yByzb5-YQNyCT9KKS0Q PsychoHypnotic].<ref>https://discord.com/channels/435431947963990026/765596827537244200/919042700215484476</ref> When dropped, it will emit Psycho's Twitch alert sound.

It can be found in two places:
* [[6 Tanglewood Drive]]: in a kitchen cupboard 
* [[Sunny Meadows Mental Institution]]: in the Bake House, 2nd from the left oven on the upper row

==Trivia==
Psycho's alert has been created by him as an expression of awe ("oh" sound) when someone subscribes or donates during his Twitch streams. The potato is a virtual face filter that Psycho has used a lot in the past, during the COVID-19 pandemic. As of 2023 he rarely uses the filter.
