The '''player''' is the main character being controlled in ''Phasmophobia''.

==Carrying items==
Non-VR players can carry up to 4 pieces of [[equipment]], including one special slot for [[Igniter|igniters]].<!-- Verify: VR players cannot open doors while holding items in both hands.-->

==Movement==
By default, the player {{Control|walks}} at 1.6 m/s, and can {{Control|sprint}} at 3 m/s for a maximum of 3 seconds. The sprint stamina regenerates at the same frequency as its depletion 2 seconds after the player stops sprinting. If the sprint stamina is completely depleted, the player must wait 5 seconds before being able to sprint again. Sprinting and player speed can be customised via custom [[difficulty]] settings. VR players also have the option of a "teleport circle" (point-and-trigger) which allows for moving a short distance at a time. The player's stamina does not decrease if the sprint key is held down without simultaneously pressing a movement key.<!-- VR players can use the teleport circle to bypass the cooldown.-->

==Interaction==
Players can {{Control|pick up}} or {{Control|interact}} with objects within 1.6 metres. They can grab [[door]]s within 3 metres.

==Death==
{{Main|Death}}
A player dies if their collision box collides with the [[ghost]] (or a "kill area"), while there is line-of-sight. Once dead, they can only pick up items that are not considered equipment, and will see the world covered in a grey fog. Dead players can sprint infinitely.

==Player model==
On the player, there are six points, three on the head and three on the legs, that are used to determine whether the ghost has line-of-sight with the player during a [[hunt]]. These are anchored to the player's point-of-view, and not the actual model that is visible. If the ghost can see either all three points on the head or all three points on the legs, the ghost is considered to have line-of-sight with the player.

Players can change between 12 characters available in the [[shop]]. Multiple players can select the same model in the same lobby.
