The '''watch''' is a permanent piece of equipment mainly used to monitor players' [[sanity]].

==Mechanics==
The watch is worn on the player's left wrist. It can be inspected at any time during the investigation by holding the {{Control|Inspect Watch}} button. In VR, the player can simply raise their left hand to view the watch.

It is not considered as an electronic equipment and therefore cannot attract the [[ghost]] during [[hunt]]s.

===Sanity bar===
[[File:WatchOnWrist.png|thumb|A player looking at their watch.]]
On the watch's screen, an outer colored circle indicates the [[sanity]] level of all players, with the used color matching the color set in the [[options]]. The color turns grey counterclockwise the more the player loses sanity. When a player [[Death|dies]], their respective color turns darker.

On [[difficulty|difficulties]] where the [[Sanity Monitor]] is broken, the circle is also disabled.

===Timer===
In the middle of the watch's screen, a timer indicates the elapsed time since an [[Exit Door|exit door]] has been opened for the first time of the [[contract]]. This can help players to keep track of [[incense]] timers, [[Fire Source|fire source]] timers, etc.

===Voice chat===
A microphone icon can be seen below the timer when the player uses '''Push-to-talk''' or '''Toggle''' [[voice chat]] modes. It is greyed out if the player's microphone is currently muted, and white if activated by holding/pressing the {{Control|local chat}} button.

==Trivia==
*The watch cannot display hours. As such, if the contract reaches the hour mark, the watch will loop back to 00:00.
