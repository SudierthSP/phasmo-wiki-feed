The '''crucifix''' is a buyable item that is used to prevent [[ghost]]s from [[hunt]]ing.

==Mechanics==
[[File:T1CrucifixRange.png|200px|thumb|right|The tier I crucifix's effective range shown while placing it.]]
A crucifix can be held in the player's hand, {{Control|thrown}}, or {{Control|placed}}; the crucifix works in all three situations. Holding the placing button will show the default effective range for the crucifix; this is a translucent sphere with a radius depending on its tier level (even if the ghost has a different effective range):
*Tier I: 3 metres
*Tier II: 4 metres
*Tier III: 5 metres

When the ghost attempts to initiate a hunt within the crucifix's range, the hunt will fail to begin, and a charge will be used. More precisely, the hunt fails if both the ghost's feet ''and'' the ghost's head (located 1.5 metres above the floor) are within the range. The ghost is then teleported back to its [[Ghost Room|favourite room]].

Each time a crucifix successfully prevents a hunt, an [[EMF]] Level 2 reading is created, with a 33% chance for this to be [[EMF Level 5]] if it is one of the ghost's types of [[evidence]]. The ghost must also wait a minimum of 25 seconds (20 seconds for a [[Demon]]) before being able to attempt other hunts.

For the [[Demon]], the crucifix's effective range is increased by 50% for each tier:
*Tier I: 4.5 metres
*Tier II: 6 metres
*Tier III: 7.5 metres

For the [[Gallu]], the crucifix's effective range is decreased by 2 metres while in its "enraged" state, and increased by 1 metre while in its "weakened" state.

The Tier I crucifix only has one charge, while tier II and tier III both have two charges. Each time it blocks a hunt attempt, a burning effect will appear, and the crucifix will glow visually before cooling down. On first use, one arm of the crucifix will disappear. On second use, more parts of the crucifix will disappear. After the crucifix is completely burnt, the crucifix will be useless and have no further effect on the ghost.

If a player-held crucifix successfully prevents a hunt attempt, it will automatically be thrown on the ground.

The crucifix will ''not'' work if:
*The ghost is not within the specified range (even if the crucifix is located in the favourite room)
*The ghost and the crucifix are not considered on the same floor
*A cursed possession is used to initiate a [[Hunt#Cursed_hunts|cursed hunt]] (except for an unused tier III crucifix, which can be used to stop a cursed hunt at the cost of ''both'' its charges)
*A hunt has already started/is in progress

The crucifix range is not limited by any obstacle such as walls and doors. This includes exterior walls, which means that it is possible for crucifixes to be used outside provided the ghost is within its range inside the investigation area. [[42 Edgefield Road]] is a good example of this, where a ghost near the front of the house, even upstairs, could possibly use up a crucifix still hanging on the truck wall.

===Rewards===
{{Main|Media}}
Taking a [[photo]] of a crucifix that has been used once or twice will yield a '''Burned Crucifix''' photo, filming a crucifix while it is burning will yield a '''Burned Crucifix''' video, and recording the sound of a crucifix while it is burning will yield a '''Burned Crucifix''' sound. These pieces of media respectively reward up to $7 and 7 XP, $12 and 12 XP, and $7 and 7 XP, depending on the equipment tier and whether there is a Unique stamp.

==Tips==
Although the crucifix works while held in-hand, it is strongly advised to place the crucifix on the ground, as this will free the player to do other objectives or hold onto more useful equipment during the investigation.

==Trivia==
*Because it has no [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crucifix corpus] (figure of the crucified Christ), the in-game item is not a true crucifix but rather just a regular cross.
*Crucifixes have an internal timer. If they are put in the inventory (i.e. not held in hand), the timer is reset. When the ghost tries to hunt within range of multiple crucifixes, it will burn the one with the greater time.
