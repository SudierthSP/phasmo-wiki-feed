The '''video feed''' (also known as ''CCTV'' or ''Camera System'') is a feature used to give players extra surveillance from inside the [[truck]], with each map having a few angles covered by video cameras already present on the location. The video feed is accessible by using the [[computer]].

The location of all CCTV cameras pre-installed will be visible on the [[Site Map]]. The CCTV camera currently displayed on the computer will also be indicated on the site map by a beam of light showing the camera's approximate facing. Video feed from [[video camera]]s will also be available on the computer, but these will not be shown on the site map.

== Trivia ==
* It is possible (though uncommon) on some maps for players to see [[Ghost Orb]]s via the CCTV cameras, if the camera happens to be able to view into the [[ghost room]]. Ghost Orbs spawn before opening the exit door, so it will be helpful if spotted beforehand.
