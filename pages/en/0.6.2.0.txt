}}'''0.6.2.0'''<ref>[https://discord.com/channels/435431947963990026/861599315386826793/966333574229925888 Development Preview: Truck Redesign Pt. 1 (Discord)]</ref>, also known as the '''Truck Overhaul''' update, was released on 10 June 2022.<ref>[https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/3339995286952592740 ''Truck Overhaul | Update v0.6.2.0'' (on Steam)]</ref> The update includes a redesign of the truck's interior, three new ghosts, changes to existing ghosts, a new voice recognition system, and other quality-of-life changes.

== Additions ==
=== Ghosts ===
;[[Moroi]]
*Evidence: Spirit Box, Ghost Writing and Freezing Temperatures<ref>[https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/3174485479387049743 Development Preview #1 | 11/05/22 (Steam)]</ref>

;[[Deogen]]
*Evidence: Spirit Box, Ghost Writing and D.O.T.S Projector<ref>[https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/3308469454272601978 Development Preview #3 | 27/05/22 (Steam)]</ref>

;[[Thaye]]
*Evidence: Ghost Orb, Ghost Writing and D.O.T.S Projector

;General
*Actual abilities of new ghosts will be revealed about 2 weeks after release

=== Maps ===
;[[Maple Lodge Campsite]]
* Campfire will now raise temperature of the area around it

== Changes ==
=== Gameplay ===
;[[Truck]]
* New interior and exterior design
* Placing objects back onto wall to be added in Custom Difficulty update
* New footstep sounds on the floor and ramp
* [[Site Map]] now uses up and down arrows for changing floor, instead of a single button

;[[Equipment]]
* Four [[lighter]]s can now be added per contract, up from 2
* Three [[Salt|salt shakers]] can now be added per contract, up from 2

=== Ghosts ===
;[[Jinn]]
* Will now leave EMF at [[fuse box]] instead of at itself when it performs its ability

;[[Mare]]
* Slightly increased chance of used ability

;[[Oni]]
* Will no longer produce "ghost mist"

=== Hunt ===
* Increased number of minimum hiding spots in multiplayer games
** 3 players: 1 additional hiding spot
** 4 players: 2 additional hiding spots

=== General ===
;New voice recognition system
* No longer requires Windows Speech Recognition
* Voice recognition microphone can now be set in-game
* Input volume visible in audio settings
* Additional microphone input options added: Voice Detector Sensitivity and Noise Suppression
** Currently only accessible through the journal
* Removed voice recognition test as it will only work when in a server

;Other
* Removed voice recognition splash screen
* Cursor will no longer lock on VR
* Rebaked lighting across all maps to help reduce light leaks and artefacts
* Replaced the Russian, Ukrainian, Turkish, Greek, and Japanese fonts to be more readable

== Bug fixes ==
* Sanity Pills can no longer be duplicated for extra uses
* Sanity Pills will now give the correct insurance amount
* You can now use the main menu boom-box in VR
* The delay when taking a photo has been removed
* Yurei can no longer drain your sanity when you are outside the location
* Yurei will no longer stop interacting if they use their ability in the Willow garage
* You can now interact with all interactable objects in VR
* The photo camera sound will now muffle correctly between floors
* You can no longer light Lighters with lit Candles
* Candles can now be lit with other lit Candles
* Candle and Lighter flames will now cast the correct shadows
* The shoulder flashlight will no longer break when closing and opening the truck door
* Fixed missing Korean and Traditional Chinese text
* The controls text in the training will no longer overlap the keybind text
* Potential fix for the VR runtime launching when playing without VR
