}}'''0.4.1.2''' is a minor update to the game released on 12 November 2021.<ref>https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/739630/view/4554822986416321515</ref> This update removes the Halloween decorations and fixes bugs.

== Changes ==
=== Gameplay ===
*New ghost types no longer have an increased chance to be selected

=== General ===
*Halloween decorations have been removed

== Bug fixes ==
*Fixed a bug where the escape the ghost during a hunt objective was not completing
*Fixed a bug where the ghost writing book was not giving evidence photos
*Fixed a bug where the sound would break inside the top floor of the cabin if you walked near the balcony door
*Fixed a bug where the ghost orbs would not change room with the ghost
