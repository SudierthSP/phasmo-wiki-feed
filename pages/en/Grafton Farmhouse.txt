|quote=A long-abandoned farmhouse, located in the middle of nowhere. Who knows what still lurks within.
}}'''Grafton Farmhouse''' is a small [[map]] in ''Phasmophobia'', and one of the two variants of the Farmhouse, alongside [[Bleasdale Farmhouse]]. This isolated, half-ruined three-story farmhouse has a spacious ground floor, a smaller second floor, and an attic. 

==Structure==
Grafton Farmhouse includes 3 bedrooms, a dining room, a library, an attic, and a workshop, among other rooms.

===Layout tips===
*On the second floor, to the left of the door to the Master Bathroom, there is a door seemingly leading to another spacious room; however, it is jammed.
*The Foyer's main light is shattered and provides intermittent illumination; consider turning on the lantern on some boxes near the stairs for much better visibility.
*The ghost has a unique interaction when near the [[mannequin]]s located in the Work Room, being able to change their pose and teleport them around the house.
*The ghost can interact with the pullstring dolls in the Child Bedroom to make them talk, change their pose, or teleport them around the house.

The following rooms count as dim rooms and hence will drain [[sanity]] with 80% of the rate, even with all room [[light]]s being on:
*Foyer
*Attic

The [[fuse box]] does not warm up the following rooms, which stay at the [[weather]]'s ambient temperature:
*Foyer
*Living Room
*Kitchen
*Utility Room
*Dining Room
*Library
*Upstairs Hallway
*Twin Bedroom
*Attic

The following rooms allow short [[Roaming|roams]] to another floor to be completed:
*Attic
