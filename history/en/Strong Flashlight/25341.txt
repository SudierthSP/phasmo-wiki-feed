|description=An upgraded version of the flashlight. Designed to help you in large areas when the lights go out.}}The '''strong flashlight''' was an item in Phasmophobia. Previously considered the direct upgrade to the [[flashlight]], it provided a much larger illumination radius, but retained the same capabilities as its weaker counterpart. Since [[0.9.0]], it is no longer a standalone item and has since been replaced with the Tier II upgrade for the flashlight.

== Usage ==
{{See also|Flashlight}}
Strong flashlights are functionally the same as regular flashlights, with the exception of an increased area of effect, and a white shade of light instead of yellow. They could be purchased from the shop for $50, starting at Level 3.
