The ID Card is a cosmetic that shows the player's in-game progress.

==Features==
The ID card shows the name of the player, the platform they're playing on (Steam, PlayStation, or Xbox), their [[experience|level]], the player's badge, and their selected character model. For the player's own ID card, the bar shows the amount of experience needed to level up. When looking at other players' ID cards, the bar is used to adjust their [[voice chat]] volume. By default, the volume is set to 50%.

===Roles===
By default, players are assigned the ''Intern'' role. Different roles can be obtained through the prestige system, holiday events, contributing to the development of the game, being a Kinetic Games Partner, or through community-related events. Players that obtain a new role will also obtain a new badge and card background that the role is linked to. The background must be displayed on a player's card to display the role.
