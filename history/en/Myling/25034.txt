}}The '''Myling''' is a type of [[ghost]] in ''Phasmophobia''. It is distinct for being more talkative, but very quiet when [[Hunt|hunting]].

==Behaviour==
===Activity===
The Myling produces a [[Paranormal Sound|paranormal sound]] on a [[Parabolic Microphone]] or a [[Sound Recorder]] every 64 - 127 seconds, instead of every 80 - 127 seconds for other ghosts.

When a paranormal sound of a Myling is within range of a sound recorder, the static noise on the recorder starts 1 metre further away from the source (5m instead of 4m for Tier 1, 6.5m instead of 5.5m for Tier 2, and 7.5m instead of 6.5m for Tier 3), which matches the recorder's detection range.<ref>[https://youtu.be/kQt9q3js_1s The difference on a Sound Recorder between a non-Myling and a Myling (on YouTube). 1 October 2026.]</ref>

===Hunt===
When hunting, a Myling's footstep and vocalization sounds will only be audible to players within 12 meters – slightly greater than the range required for electronic interference – instead of the usual 20 meters. The volume of footsteps is slightly muffled near the limit, but quickly becomes discernible as it approaches.

==Strategies==
===Identification===
The best way to identify a Myling is taking advantage of the small difference between its audible range (12m) and the electronic interference range (10m), and having a rough intuition of these ranges. If the ghost's sounds are very faint or not audible at all at the threshold of the interference range, then it is probably a Myling. Note that this test is not valid if the ghost is on a different floor, since this will muffle the audible range. This can be tested in multiple ways, such as:
*Before a hunt, dropping an active electronic equipment at your preferred hiding spot, then looking for the behaviour during the hunt
*Holding an active electronic equipment or using {{Control|Global Chat}} during a hunt, with [[incense]] for protection

Note that the [[Raiju]] has a larger interference range (15m), which may create a similar effect. Use other methods (such as ghost speed) to eliminate the Raiju.

;Paranormal sounds
If two paranormal sounds are picked up within 80 seconds on a Parabolic Microphone or a Sound Recorder, then it is a Myling.

When a paranormal sound is within detection range of the Sound Recorder, check if the static noise starts at the maximum distance at which the recorder can detect sounds or 1 metre closer to the sound's source. If it is the former, it is a Myling. If it is the latter, it is not a Myling. Note that this test is more easily done with a Tier 1 or Tier 3 recorder.

===Survival===
On larger maps, especially in long hallways, it is prudent to visually look out for the ghost, as its reduced audible range gives it a mild advantage in catching a player off guard, while also gaining speed for longer if line-of-sight was achieved; alternatively, a Parabolic Microphone renders this a non-issue, as it will detect the ghost's footsteps and vocalizations if it is in range, regardless of whether it's a Myling or not.

==Trivia==
*In Scandinavian folklore, Mylings (pronounced “mai-ling” { /ˈmaɪlɪŋ/ } or “me-ling” { /ˈmiːlɪŋ/ } for a more traditional pronunciation) are ghosts of children who were either killed by their parents, unbaptised, or abandoned. Because they were not baptized, Mylings cannot rest and are forced to roam until they can persuade a living being to carry them or their body to hallowed grounds where they can rest peacefully. Failure to meet a Myling’s demands will result in them killing the benefactor. In-game, the Myling's age and ghost model are randomly chosen, and the ghost does not have to be of a young age.
*Another way that the Myling can find peace is if it can find and kill its mother. Usually it needs to be either carried by someone or get a form of "permission" to move/search from a relative in order to move from its burial place. This permission is usually obtained by trickery or vague wording of the request. They are usually depicted as small children in a early stage of decay (swollen and discoloured skin) but can choose to appear as adult if that was the age they would have been had they been alive.
