The '''ability''' state is a state that allows the [[ghost]] to perform further actions.

==Mechanics==
After the ghost enters this state through the [[activity]] check, it will be able to choose one of the following three actions:
*Interact with the [[fuse box]]
*Use its ability proper (such as the [[Phantom]]'s travel ability or the [[Demon]]'s hunt ability)
*Perform a [[ghost event]]

If the ghost is trapped due to the [[Monkey Paw]]'s 'trap wish', or if the ghost is a [[Shade]] and there is a player in the same room as it, it will enter the [[Ghost Room|favourite room]] state instead of continuing in the ability state.

While in the ability state, the ghost will roll an integer between 0 and 11. If the ghost is a [[Thaye]], it will instead roll a float between 0.0 and 12.0 multiplied by its activity modifier (between 0.5 and 2 depending on its age), rounded down to the nearest integer.

Depending on the [[difficulty]] settings, the rolled number will get one reroll:
*If ''Event frequency'' is Low and the number is ≥ 7
*If ''Event frequency'' is High and the number is ≤ 6
There is an additional reroll if the [[weather]] is Blood Moon and if the number is ≤ 6
