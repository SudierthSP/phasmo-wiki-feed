{{Quote
|speaker=Loading blurb
|quote=Shut down due to unnatural deaths, this isolated school has been abandoned for decades.
}}'''Brownstone High School''' is a [[map]] in ''Phasmophobia''. It was the first medium-sized map added to the game, though it is now a large-sized map. Its square shape and general symmetry can make it difficult to navigate, and its long hallways may make perpetual running almost impossible.

==Structure==
Brownstone High School has one front and one side yard (separated from each other), about three dozen classrooms, eight bathrooms, a lecture hall, a cafeteria, and a gymnasium complete with two locker rooms. The entire building is approximately 85 by 55 meters. 

Brownstone High School features five [[exit door]]s. Due to the large distance between the closest entrance and the [[truck]], it may be desirable to stage gear close to the building before entering. The rear exit doors lead onto a outdoor yard featuring an exterior basketball court. It is considered a safe zone, therefore preventing passive sanity drain.

===Layout tips===
*There are four pairs of bathrooms on the map, with one pair per side per floor:
**All male bathrooms are on the left, and all female bathrooms are on the right.
**Each pair consists of a student's bathroom (labelled boys/girls) and a teacher's bathroom (labelled male/female). The teacher's bathroom is behind the "regular" bathroom (when looking towards the back).
*Classrooms numbered 20 and above are all located on the '''2'''nd floor. The rest, classrooms 1-19, are downstairs.
*In addition to doors and windows, [[Ultraviolet#Fingerprints|fingerprints]] can also appear on bathroom mirrors.

The following rooms cannot be chosen as a [[ghost room|favourite room]]:

Due to the very large size of the map, it may not even be necessary to hide if the player is sufficiently far from the ghost. The player can simply sit in a room far away from the ghost, as it would be extremely unlikely for it to wander by chance to the player's location if it has not detected them. 

On the other hand, the large size of the map may make noticing the start of a hunt much more difficult. This can be particularly dangerous in hallways, as the player may be taken by surprise by a ghost that started hunting from far away. Communication between players and vigilance is key to avoid bad surprises; keeping exit doors open to hear or see them close and periodically trying to toggle light switches can help players know if a hunt started elsewhere in the building.

The ghost is able to walk through many of the teacher's desks found in the classrooms, rendering them not useful for trying to loop the ghost.

==Brownstone High School - Restricted==
The restricted version of the map can be selected independently of the main location, and is considered a medium map. In the first-floor Restricted versions, only the Foyer (where the [[Cursed Possession]]s spawn) and one randomly-selected front quarter of the lower floor will be able to be accessed (including the side entrance corresponding to the variant), with the other side entrance being blocked off. In the second-floor Restricted versions, the main entrance will always be blocked, with only one side entrance available (marked by exterior lighting). Only the staircase on the lower level can be accessed (where the Cursed Possessions spawn bar the [[Summoning Circle]]),  and one randomly-selected front quarter of the upper floor will be able to be accessed, with the Summoning Circle spawning to one side of the top of the stairs.

==Bone locations==
Bones spawn randomly, but will only spawn in areas that a ghost can also walk on, within a room that can be chosen as a [[Ghost Room|favourite room]]. Additionally, the bone cannot spawn in '''Lobby''', '''Basketball Court''', '''Cafeteria''', and '''Lecture Hall'''.

==Trivia==
*Despite being larger than [[Sunny Meadows Mental Institution]]—the other Large map—in terms of playable area, Brownstone High School was classified as a Medium map until [[0.9.0]].<ref>https://discord.com/channels/435431947963990026/765596827537244200/1142680133933748254</ref>
**This makes it the first map to have changed size class between versions of the game.
*The "Second Floor Main Corridor" is one of the largest rooms in the map, competing with "Basketball Court". Both rooms individually are also among the largest rooms in the game.
*The assets used for this map can be found [https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/urban/hq-abandoned-school-modular-106665 here].
*There is a phone in every classroom that the ghost can ring as an interaction. In earlier versions of the game, this ringing was very loud and could be heard from great distances.
