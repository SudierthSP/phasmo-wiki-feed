The '''sound recorder''' is a device used to record [[Media#Sound evidence|sound evidence]].

==Mechanics==
The Sound Recorder can be {{Control|toggled}} on to begin scanning the area for any noise disturbance produced by the ghost or to record [[Media#Sound evidence|sound evidence]]. Up to 3 audio clips can be stored in the [[journal]] per [[contract]]. The recorder picks up ghost-related sounds such as [[Paranormal Sound|paranormal sounds]] or [[ghost event]]s, as well as evidence-related sounds such as [[Spirit Box]] responses, [[Ghost Writing]], or [[EMF Level 5]].

When the recorder detects a valid noise, its sound meter will start to rise, indicating to the player how close they are to the source of the sound. The sound detection range depends on the tier:
*Tier I: 5 metres
*Tier II: 6.5 metres
*Tier III: 7.5 metres
For paranormal sounds, the sound meter/static noise will start to rise 1 metre below the ranges mentioned above (unless the ghost is a [[Myling]]).

To record a sound, the player must hold the {{Control|Use}} button when sufficiently close to the source. This is indicated by the meter lights turning green on a Tier I, the power spectrum reaching the upper bar on a Tier II, and the circle at the center of the compass turning solid on a Tier III. Once a valid sound is captured, the recording will stop automatically. The recording range depends on the tier:
*Tier I: 1 metre
*Tier II: 1.5 metres
*Tier III: 2 metres
"Hunting Ghost", "Ghost Event", and "Music Box" sounds can be recorded from further away.

If a sound is considered as a Duplicate, the Tier I's lights will be less bright and the Tier II and III's power spectrums will be faded when approaching the source.
