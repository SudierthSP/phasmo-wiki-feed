'''Mannequins''' are props that the [[ghost]] can interact with. They are most commonly found in garages and basements.

==Mechanics==
Players cannot interact with or move dress forms/mannequins in any way.

;Dress Form
Dress forms can either be rotated or teleported elsewhere at fixed spots by the ghost. When the dress form is teleported, the [[EMF]] reading will be located at the dress form's previous location.

;Mannequin
Similarly to the dress form, when the ghost [[Interaction|interacts]] with a mannequin, it can either:
*change its pose (51% chance)
*teleport it elsewhere (30% chance)
*rotate its head (19% chance)

When the ghost attempts to change the mannequin's pose or teleport it elsewhere, it checks if a player (including dead players) is looking at the mannequin every 0.5 seconds. The interaction will succeed if no player is looking at it, and will fail if there is still a player looking at it after 10 seconds.

The pose changing will also make the mannequin face the nearest player.

When the mannequin is teleported, it will be teleported to a spot on the [[bone]] navmesh in a random player's room, that is further than 0.8 metres away from that player, and outside of their field of view. The teleportation will always change the mannequin's pose, but will never leave an EMF reading.
