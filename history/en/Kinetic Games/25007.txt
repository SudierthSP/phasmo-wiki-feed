==History==
===2018===
Daniel Knight (Dknighter) is believed to have begun work on ''Spectrophobia'' from as early as 2018, with the earliest changelog available for the game in early-alpha dating back to December 2019. On 3 March 2020, the game was renamed ''Phasmophobia'', and the company was renamed to Kinetic Games. It is unknown what the company was named prior.

===2021===
Before mid-2021, a person known as Botsero on the official Phasmophobia Discord was the main tester and idea curator for the game, though he was technically not considered part of Kinetic Games. Botsero has since retired.

===2026===
On 8 January 2026, Kinetic Games has announced the launch of '''Kinetic Publishing''', its new publishing label, aiming to champion innovation within the independent game development space, and help small teams and solo developers on games across a wide range of genres.<ref>https://www.kineticgames.co.uk/news/announcing-kinetic-publishing</ref>
