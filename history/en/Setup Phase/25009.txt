The '''setup phase''' is the starting phase of a game. During this phase, players can explore the location, collect and set up [[equipment|equipments]], and locate the [[ghost]] before it can initiate a [[hunt]].

== Mechanics ==
The duration of the setup phase depends on the [[difficulty]]:

Countdown of the setup phase is shown on the [[Setup Timer|timer]] in the [[truck]] above the [[computer]], and will only begin when any [[Exit Door|entryway]] to the building is opened.

During the setup phase, [[sanity]] decay due to being in darkness is reduced by roughly 30%, and sanity cannot go below 50%. Additionally, if the ghost attempts to initiate a hunt on its own through its hunt sanity threshold or any abilities it may have, it will enter the [[Ghost Room|favourite room state]] instead of hunting. Other than these conditions, the setup phase does not affect the ghost's behaviour or other game mechanics. When the time runs out, the ghost will be allowed to initiate a hunt, depending on the ghost type and the players' average sanity, and the sanity drain rate will increase to normal.

The setup phase can be ended early by activating or using any [[cursed possession]]. There is currently a bug where the [[Ouija Board]] cancels the setup phase only if it recognizes a valid phrase instead of upon activation.

== Tips ==
*The setup time is valuable for newer players in setting up equipment, looking for evidence, and scouting hiding spots before the ghost is permitted to hunt.
*Having the setup phase end does not mean that the ghost will immediately begin hunting, as whether ghosts can hunt is generally dependent on whether the average team [[sanity]] is below a certain threshold. Most of the time, this threshold will not have been met by the time the setup phase ends.
