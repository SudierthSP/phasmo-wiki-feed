|quote=A newly built home in an established area. Reports say it was built on top of something unearthly.
}}'''13 Willow Street''' (or simply '''Willow''') is a [[map]] in ''Phasmophobia''. It is a small, single-story modern home with one bedroom and bathroom, as well as a home gym and a fitted out basement. It is one of the smallest maps in the game, being only slightly larger than [[6 Tanglewood Drive]], but feels almost tiny due to its spacious open rooms, a sharp contrast to the generally congested environments of the other suburban homes.

Willow can often make for a swift investigation due to its small size, but the lack of lockers and easily-accessible side rooms to hide in makes it relatively dangerous during a [[hunt]]. As such, players will have to learn the ins and outs of hiding behind furniture in order to survive.

==Structure==
Willow consists of one bedrooms, one bathroom, a gym room, and a large basement divided into several fitted-out rooms, among other rooms.
