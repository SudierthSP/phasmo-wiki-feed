}}'''0.20.0''', also known as the '''Resonance''' update, is a major update released on 24 November 2026.<ref>[https://www.kineticgames.co.uk/news/phasmophobia-v02000-resonance-patch-notes ''Phasmophobia v0.20.0.0 - Resonance Patch Notes''. 24 November 2026.]</ref><ref group="note">Official Kinetic Games Partners were given early access keys on 23 November 2026.</ref> This update moves the game's engine to Unity 6.

==Additions==
*'''Unity 6:''' The move to Unity 6 brings with it improvements across the board for Phasmophobia including performance, stability, lighting and more. Phasmophobia also now supports various different quality presets for in-game systems and different platforms such as Steam Deck. Importantly, this update brings with it a host of bug fixes, all of which can be found below.

*'''Performance Improvements & New Options:''' Unity 6 and its accompanying features has improved Phasmophobia’s performance and stability through notable changes. Resonance addresses commonly encountered crashes, improves CPU, GPU, VRAM and RAM usage as well as allowing for new overall quality presets. Elsewhere, a new Extreme shadow setting for compatible hardware has been added and an option to turn off/on the Live Camera Feed (for Photo & Video Cameras) is now available. ''Note: If Live Camera Feed is turned off, the Media highlight will still appear on the Camera.''

*'''Lighting Improvements:''' Resonance brings with it an overhaul to the overall lighting system in Phasmophobia. These changes will be notable during all weather types where darker areas of a location will be lit through windows and doorways, and outside areas will be brighter. Areas of improvement also cover lighting on dynamic objects such as props and doors; originally, an interactable would be lit in a different way to others whereas now these will blend with the rest of the environment.

*'''Voice Recognition Improvements & Changes:''' Additions were made to Voice Recognition to allow for a more immersive experience with new options allowing players the chance to ask for interactions or proof of presence from the Ghost. An additional page in the Journal has been added too, listing off various line options. These new options replace the ability to say certain words or the Ghost’s name to try and increase Ghost activity.

*'''Sound Sensor Map:''' Once a Sound Sensor has been placed down inside a location, an icon on the Truck’s map will reflect the type of sound as and when it happens. These will ping differently depending on if the sound is a single sound or a looping sound. A sound can only be picked up if it is happening within the range of the Sensor itself. Additional sounds have also been added to what can be picked up by a Sound Sensor.

*'''[[Dowsing Rods]]:''' The Dowsing Rods are a new piece of Equipment used to locate the Bone during any Contract. Designed mainly for use on larger maps, the Dowsing Rods will point players towards the Bone, with higher tiers providing better accuracy such as which floor the Bone can be found on.

*'''Map Voting:''' The Map Voting system has been reworked to allow for a more customised approach for players. Players are now able to select as many maps as they wish to create their own random map pool. Buttons on the Contract board can be used to select all small, medium and large maps separately as well as a randomiser that works in the same way as the original Random polaroid did.

*'''Colour Blind options:''' Various different Colour Blind options are now available. These can now be found within the Settings > Video menu and support the following types of colour blindness: Protanopia, Protanomaly, Deuteranopia, Deuteranomaly, Tritanopia, Tritanomaly, Achromatopsia, Achromatomaly.

*'''In-Game Warnings:''' A new Warning system has been implemented. Initially, these will provide warnings for violations of our Code of Conduct. If action is taken against a report, the player who made the report will now receive a notification informing them that it has been successful.

*'''XBOX Play Anywhere:''' Phasmophobia is now available as part of the XBOX Play Anywhere program, allowing players to play the game across the devices that support XBOX - PC, laptops, ROG Ally and XBOX Series X/S. Save data will carry across your devices, meaning you can switch between any XBOX system and retain your progress.

*'''Console language support:''' Additional language support for PlayStation 5 and XBOX Series X/S has been added. The following languages are now supported on all platforms: English, German, French, Italian, Bulgarian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, Finnish, Greek, Hungarian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Polish, Romanian, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Swedish, Traditional Chinese, Turkish, Ukrainian, Catalan, Arabic, Spanish (Latin America), Portuguese (Portugal), Spanish (Spain), Portuguese (Brazil).

*'''Eye Adaptation:''' A new Eye Adaptation setting, Performance, has been added for all players, including VR. The previous On option is now changed to Quality. If Eye Adaptation was previously set to On, it will now be set to Quality. If it was Off, it will remain Off. ''Note: VR and low end hardware only support the Performance setting.''

==Changes==
;Ghosts
*A Yurei will now open and close doors during Hunts.
*A Yurei’s normal interactions with doors are now slightly faster than other Ghost types.
*During a Hunt, a Yurei moves doors faster than other Ghost types.
*A Poltergeist now has a chance to either throw or interact with objects, rather than only throwing, while still retaining a higher chance to throw objects than other Ghost types.
*A Goryo will no longer change rooms if the ‘’I Wish For Sanity’’ Monkey Paw wish is used.

;General
*In Settings, ‘Cursor Color’ has been changed to ‘Reticle Color’.
*Players using controllers can now switch CCTV tabs in the Truck with DPAD arrows.
*The Contracts board now reads as ‘Vote on Contracts’ due to the addition of improved map voting options.
*Players who have received a Ban will now have their Cross-play settings locked to ‘Disabled’.
*PC players now have the option to choose between Performance & Quality modes for Eye Adaptation. If the player's hardware is unable to support Quality, this will be automatically set to Performance. ''Note: Consoles will have this automatically set to Performance.''
*The Truck map will now display the relevant map if on a Restricted variant.
*Players on consoles will now be able to change their Cursor color.
*Instead of being presented with a ‘Game Full’ message after being invited, the player will now be informed the current Contract is ongoing and to attempt to join once it is finished.
*When calibrating height in PSVR2, ‘Trigger’ has been changed to ‘’L2 and R2’’.
