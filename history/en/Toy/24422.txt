'''Toys''' are props which the [[ghost]] can interact with.

==Mechanics==
[[File:Teddy Bear.png|right|120x120px|thumb|A Teddy bear in [[Sunny Meadows Mental Institution|Sunny Meadows]].]]
;Teddy bears
When a ghost [[Interaction|interacts]] with a teddy bear, one of four things will happen: it will throw the bear, make it giggle, twist its head by 180 degrees, or teleport it to another location.

[[File:Bunny.png|right|120x120px|thumb|A bunny in [[10 Ridgeview Court]].]]
;Bunnies
Contrary to teddy bears, ghosts can only throw or teleport bunnies when interacting with them.

[[File:Doll.png|right|120x120px|thumb|A doll in [[Grafton Farmhouse]].]]
;Dolls
On [[Grafton Farmhouse]], dolls can be found in Child Bedroom. When a ghost interacts with them, it can either make them talk, change their pose, or teleport them elsewhere, usually near the player.

[[ru:Игрушки]]
[[File:Parrot.png|right|120x120px]]
[[File:Shark.png|right|120x120px|thumb|From top: a parrot and a shark in [[Point Hope]].]]
;Parrot & Sharks
On [[Point Hope]], a parrot and two sharks can be found in Child Bedroom. When a ghost interacts with them, it can either throw them or play a short sound. Players can also play the short sound by using the {{Control|Primary Use}} button while holding these toys.

===Rewards===
{{Main|Media}}
Recording a teddy bear giggling will yield a '''Possessed Toy''' sound in the journal, rewarding up to $7 and 7 XP, depending on the tier of the [[Sound Recorder]] and whether there is a Unique stamp.
