'''Equipment''' in ''[[Phasmophobia]]'' is used to determine the type of [[ghost]] inhabiting a location, or to complete optional [[objectives]]. Players can purchase equipment using money they receive from successful [[contract|investigations]].

==Mechanics==
Equipment may be bought or sold in the [[Shop]] when in the [[lobby]].

When a player dies, they lose all equipment they added to the mission, excluding the default starter equipment that is always provided. Players who stay alive do not have to collect their equipment at the end of a contract to keep them; they will be returned automatically. Items categorized as consumable (e.g. incense and sanity medication) do not need to be bought again if the player remains alive, but if the player uses them, they need to be rebought.

Players can buy and store a maximum of 999 of each item.

Ghosts disrupt nearby electronic equipment whenever they are visible, such as during a [[hunt]] or when they manifest (i.e. become visible while ''not'' hunting). Holding active electronic equipment during a hunt will attract nearby ghosts.

There are 3 types of equipment:
*[[Equipment#Starter_equipment|Starter equipment]]
*[[Equipment#Optional_equipment|Optional equipment]]
*[[Equipment#Truck_equipment|Truck equipment]]

==Consumable equipment==
Some pieces of equipment are consumable, and will need to be re-purchased after they have been used on an investigation:
*[[Crucifix]] (All Tiers)
*[[Firelight]] (Tier I, Tier II)
*[[Igniter]] (Tier I, Tier II)
*[[Incense]] (Tier I, Tier II)
*[[Sanity Medication]] (All Tiers)
*[[Salt]] (All Tiers)

==Removed equipment==
;[[Infrared Light Sensor]]
The infrared light sensor detected movement from both ghosts and players and would illuminate the area with a bright light, much like the current [[Motion Sensor]]. It however would not provide feedback to the truck, nor count in the "Detect the Ghost with a Motion Sensor" objective. This item was removed in [[0.3.0]].

==Trivia==
*To buy enough equipment to bring the maximum amount of each to a contract, players will need to spend $2,130.
*To buy one of each equipment, players will need to spend $805.
*The motion sensor is the most expensive item in the game with a price of $100. Meanwhile, the igniter is the cheapest with a price of $10.
