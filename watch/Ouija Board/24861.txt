''"The most common way of accidentally summoning a ghost is with a Ouija Board, so you may come across these during your investigations. Be careful if you choose to use it as they have been known to lower your sanity. To use one, activate it and then ask a question with your voice."''

The '''Ouija Board''' is a [[Cursed Possession|cursed possession]] in ''Phasmophobia''. It can be used to communicate with the [[ghost]] at the cost of [[sanity]].

==Mechanics==
A Ouija Board has a 1 in 7 chance per contract of being chosen as the cursed possession that will spawn, with one specific spawn location per map.

A Ouija Board can be {{Control|picked up}} with an empty hand. Swapping to another item will cause the Ouija Board to drop from the inventory.

===Activation===
The Ouija Board can be activated by {{Control|interacting}} with it if the player is not holding it, or {{Control|toggling}} it directly while holding the board. The planchette<ref group="note">A planchette; an instrument used in combination with a ouija board to communicate by the ghost 'moving' the instrument to the letters of its desired responses</ref> will drop down onto the board, signifying that the board has been activated. There is currently a bug where an ongoing [[setup phase]] will only be cancelled if a valid phrase has been recognized and not upon activation of the board.

===Questioning===
[[File:Ouija Board text UI.jpg|thumb|200px|The Ouija Board's textual UI.]]
Players can choose to either use voice chat or a text-based UI to ask questions.
*If using voice chat, once activated, the Ouija Board will respond to [[voice chat|voice input]] within 5 meters of it on the same floor.
*If using the text-based UI, the UI will appear upon activation. Players can then click to select one of 3 categories, then select a category-specific question, or say goodbye. The player cannot exit the UI without saying goodbye or the board breaking, but the board can be thrown out while holding it; this automatically deactivates the board.

The player asking the question must be within the investigation area to ask questions, but does not have to be in the [[ghost room]] (nor the ghost's current room).

If the question is recognized, the planchette will move to answer the question, and an [[EMF]] Level 2 reading will be generated around the Ouija Board. If the ghost has [[EMF Level 5]] as evidence, there is a 33% chance for it to appear per question asked.

Whether a ghost is "shy" or not does not affect its ability to respond through the Ouija Board. If the Ouija Board is asked a question but nothing happens, then either the board is not activated or the phrase was not recognized. There is a cooldown of ~2.5 seconds for clicking via text-based UI, while there is no cooldown time for voice chat, aside from any voice recognition limitations.

===Deactivation===
[[File:Broken Ouija Board.jpg|thumb|202x202px|A broken Ouija Board]]
To deactivate the Ouija Board, any nearby player must say "Goodbye", which will make the planchette disappear. The Ouija Board can then be left alone safely. The Ouija Board will also deactivate automatically if the player holds the board and exits the investigation area.

The Ouija board will burn and break apart, initiating a [[Hunt#Cursed hunt|cursed hunt]], if:
*There is no player within 5 meters of a still-active board (leaving this range applies)
*The player currently asking questions has less sanity than required to "pay" for the question
*The phrase "hide and seek" (or similar) is said (see [[#Questions|Questions]])
If there are no players inside when the board breaks, it will not trigger a hunt, but will still extend the duration of later hunts.

===Rewards===
{{Main|Media}}
Using a [[Photo Camera]] to take a photo of the Ouija Board will yield a photo labelled as '''Ouija Board''', and reward up to $7 and 7 XP.

When the ghost answers a question by moving the planchette, a '''Ouija Board''' video and a '''Ouija Board''' sound can be captured using a [[Video Camera]] and a [[Sound Recorder]]. Each of these pieces of media will reward up to $7 and 7 XP.

==Questions==
There are at least 13 different types of questions (including two jokes) that can be asked:
*Location
*Proximity
*Age
*Death
*Room count
*Sanity
*Method of death
*Bone location
*Hide and seek
*Feeling
*Purpose
*Joke
*Shyness

Note that if a question does not get a response, it is not considered a valid question. A few example questions:
*"Are you from [Country/State Name]?"
*"Are you [single/married]?"
*"What is your name?"

==Tips==
*You can quickly and precisely decrease your sanity (and, by extension, the average sanity of your team) by asking 5% sanity drain questions with the Ouija Board repeatedly, even while it's in the middle of answering another question; this can be helpful for testing hunt sanity thresholds.
*You can repeatedly ask low-cost questions with the Ouija Board in order to create numerous interactions; with an [[EMF Reader]] nearby, EMF level 5 reading may be quickly attained, as each question has a 33% chance to give off the evidence.
*When using voice chat to ask questions, wait at least a few seconds for the board to respond before asking again, to avoid breaking the board when asking high sanity-deducting questions.
*When using the text UI and asking for "Hide and seek", be mindful that you will only exit the UI once the board breaks after the countdown.
