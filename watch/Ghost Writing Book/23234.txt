}}The '''Ghost Writing Book''' is a piece of [[equipment]] used to obtain [[Ghost Writing]] by placing it near a [[ghost]].

==Usage==
===Placement===
A translucent preview of the Ghost Writing Book opened will appear while the player is aiming at a surface and holding down the {{Control|Secondary Use}} button. The book will be placed when the button is released.

===Function===
If [[Ghost Writing]] is a piece of evidence, the ghost will try to write in a book every 45 to 90 seconds and the chance to succeed depends on the ghost writing book tier: 51%, 67%, 76% for tiers 1, 2, 3, respectively. The timer starts as soon as the equipment is loaded and is different for each book.

When the interaction succeeds, the book will slightly levitate and the pencil/pen will start to frantically scribble on the pages visually and audibly, followed by the pencil/pen being thrown on the ground. An [[EMF]] Level 2 reading is also created, with a 33% chance of it being [[EMF Level 5]] instead if this is one of the ghost's types of [[evidence]]. The ghost will not write in the book if it is closed and/or out of range.

A ghost can throw the Ghost Writing Book only if it does not have Ghost Writing among its types of evidence or when that piece of evidence is hidden. After being thrown, the book will close.

===Rewards===
{{Main|Media}}
Taking a photo of the Ghost Writing Book after it has been written in will yield a photo labelled as '''Ghost Writing''', and reward up to $12 and 12 XP depending on the tier of the [[Photo Camera]] and whether there is a Unique stamp. A video and a sound labelled as '''Ghost Writing''', each awarding up to $12 and 12 XP, can also be recorded when the ghost is writing in the book.

==Strategies==
*If placed within the vision of an active [[Video Camera|video camera]], you can use the [[Video Feed]] in the truck to watch ghost writing books and see if ghost writing is occurring. While it may be much more difficult to see if there are any markings in the pages on camera, more observant players may check if the pen is missing from the book to determine if ghost writing has occurred or not.
*A nearby [[Sound Sensor|sound sensor]] can also be used to monitor a Ghost Writing Book from the truck; this will allow players to hear the sound of Ghost Writing occuring without having to actively monitor the book.
